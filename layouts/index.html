Did you know?
  • … that a yield curve inverts when short-term yields rise above long-term yields — a pattern that has preceded most US recessions since 1955?
  • … that the modern Federal Reserve operates without explicit appropriations from Congress because it funds itself from the income on its bond portfolio?
  • … that more than half of all US household stock ownership is now held through low-cost index funds and ETFs — a structural shift only a generation old?
  • … that the average individual hedge fund charges roughly twenty times what an index fund does, yet most fail to beat the market over a full cycle?

How to use this wiki

This is a reading wiki, not a textbook. Click any link to follow the thread you're curious about — every entry was written assuming you might arrive cold. Use the A–Z index to browse all entries, the search box to find a specific term.

For long-form learning paths, see the sibling project Pomegra Learn — a 30-book curriculum that takes the same ground in chapter form.

Pomegra Wiki is part of Pomegra's free financial-education effort. All entries are written for general readers; nothing here is investment advice.
