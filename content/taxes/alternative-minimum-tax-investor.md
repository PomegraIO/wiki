*The **Alternative Minimum Tax** (AMT) is a parallel federal tax system designed to ensure that high-income earners pay a minimum amount of tax. Taxpayers with high income or certain deductions (like large [depreciation](/depreciation-recapture-investor/), [private activity bonds](/interest-rate/), or concentrated [capital gains](/capital-gains-tax-investor/)) must calculate both regular tax and AMT, then pay whichever is higher. For affected investors, AMT can increase the effective tax rate by several percentage points.*

## How AMT works

You calculate tax in two ways:

**Regular tax**: Using standard brackets and deductions (what most people calculate).

**AMT**: Starting with "Alternative Minimum Taxable Income" (AMTI), which adds back certain deductions and preferences, then applies a flat 26%-28% rate.

You pay the **higher** of the two.

## Example: regular vs. AMT

Suppose your regular [taxable income](/tax-bracket-investor/) is $300,000 (marginal rate 35%, tax ~$105,000).

You have $100,000 in [depreciation deductions](/depreciation-recapture-investor/) (say, from a rental property partnership via [K-1 income](/k-1-investor/)).

For **AMT**, you add back the $100,000 depreciation, making AMTI = $400,000. At 28% AMT rate, AMT = $112,000.

Regular tax: $105,000. AMT: $112,000. You pay the higher: **$112,000 AMT**.

The $7,000 difference is the cost of AMT—it increased your tax despite the [depreciation](/depreciation-recapture-investor/) deduction.

## AMTI and preferences

Alternative Minimum Taxable Income starts with regular [taxable income](/tax-bracket-investor/) and adds back certain "preference items":

- [Depreciation](/depreciation-recapture-investor/) (difference between regular and AMT depreciation)
- Intangible drilling costs
- Tax-exempt interest on [private activity bonds](/interest-rate/)
- [Incentive stock option](/option/) gains
- Certain [charitable contributions](/charitable-contribution-deduction/)

For investors, the big one is [depreciation](/depreciation-recapture-investor/), especially [K-1 income](/k-1-investor/) from real estate partnerships.

## The AMT exemption

There is an exemption amount (roughly $85,925 for single filers, $133,500 for married, in 2024) that reduces AMTI before the 26%-28% rate is applied. This exemption phases out as AMTI rises, making the effective AMT rate higher for very high earners.

## Long-term capital gains and AMT

Good news: [long-term capital gains](/long-term-capital-gain-tax/) are taxed at preferential rates under AMT, just like regular tax. This is rare—most tax preferences are bad under AMT, but [long-term gains](/long-term-capital-gain-tax/) are taxed at 0%, 15%, or 20% under both systems.

## Who is affected

AMT is most common for:
- High-income earners ($500,000+)
- Investors in real estate partnerships with large [depreciation deductions](/depreciation-recapture-investor/)
- [K-1 investors](/k-1-investor/)
- Those with [incentive stock options](/option/)
- Residents of high-tax states (state tax is an [AMT preference](/alternative-minimum-tax-investor/))

## Form 6251

AMT is calculated on Form 6251, filed with the return. If your calculation shows AMT higher than regular tax, Form 6251 is required; otherwise, it is optional (though recommended to track).

Your tax software will calculate it automatically if you have preference items.

## AMT credit and carryforward

If you pay AMT in one year because of timing preferences (like [depreciation](/depreciation-recapture-investor/), which creates a difference between years), you can claim an **AMT credit** in later years when regular tax exceeds AMT. This credit can refund some of the AMT you overpaid.

The credit is complex to calculate; most tax software handles it.

## Mitigation strategies

If you are subject to AMT:

1. **Spread [depreciation](/depreciation-recapture-investor/)**. If you can choose between accelerated and straight-line [depreciation](/depreciation-recapture-investor/), straight-line may reduce AMT.
2. **Avoid [preference items](/alternative-minimum-tax-investor/)**. Be cautious about [private activity bonds](/interest-rate/) or [incentive stock options](/option/).
3. **Timing [capital gains](/capital-gains-tax-investor/)**. AMT does not penalize [long-term gains](/long-term-capital-gain-tax/), so [gains](/capital-gains-tax-investor/) are less harmful under AMT than ordinary income.
4. **Consult a [tax professional](/schedule-d/)**. AMT is complex; professional guidance can identify the best strategy.

## Historical note

AMT was enacted to prevent high-income earners from using deductions to avoid tax entirely. However, due to a failure to index the exemption for inflation (until 2013), AMT increasingly affects middle-income taxpayers in high-tax states. The exemption is now indexed, limiting the reach.
