*The **Leverage Shares 2X Long ONDS Daily ETF** (ONDG) is a leveraged exchange-traded fund designed to amplify daily price movements of Ondas Holdings, a wireless technology company trading on the [Nasdaq](/nasdaq/). ONDG resets its leverage daily, which makes it a tactical tool for short-term traders rather than a long-term holding.*

## What is ONDG, and what does it track?

ONDG is a [leveraged ETF](/leveraged-etf/) that targets two times the daily percentage return of Ondas Holdings stock. If ONDS rises 1 percent on a trading day, ONDG aims to gain approximately 2 percent; if ONDS falls 1 percent, ONDG targets a 2 percent loss. The fund uses derivatives—swaps, [futures contracts](/futures-contract/), and other instruments—to create this leverage synthetically rather than buying the stock on margin. The fund does not hold ONDS shares but instead replicates the 2x movement through financial engineering, resetting that leverage position at the close of each trading day.

## How does the daily reset mechanism work, and why does it matter?

Each night at market close, ONDG recalibrates its derivatives to match exactly 2x the previous day's close of ONDS. This reset is crucial for understanding how the fund behaves. On any single day, ONDG delivers its promised 2x multiplier faithfully. But over multiple days, the daily rebalancing creates a mathematical drag called volatility decay. A stock that gains 5 percent one day and loses 5 percent the next ends flat; a 2x leveraged fund tracking that pattern would gain 10 percent the first day, then lose 10 percent the second, finishing down about 1 percent. The daily reset forces the fund to rebalance in a way that locks in this compounding loss, especially in choppy or sideways markets. The longer the [holding period](/holding-period/), the more pronounced the erosion becomes.

## Who sponsors ONDG and how is it structured?

Leverage Shares, a London-based firm specializing in leveraged and [inverse ETFs](/inverse-etf/), issues and manages ONDG. The fund trades on exchange as a standard ETF (on [NYSE Arca](/nyse-arca/) or similar venues depending on geography), giving it transparent pricing and exchange-like liquidity. Structurally, it is an exchange-traded note backed by derivatives rather than a traditional ETF holding a basket of stocks. This means investors face sponsor [credit risk](/credit-risk/): if Leverage Shares or the fund's counterparties fail, the ETN could lose value. This structural nuance is important for understanding the full risk profile.

## What are ONDG's costs, and how liquid is it?

ONDG charges an [expense ratio](/expense-ratio/) that covers fund management and the ongoing cost of maintaining its derivative positions. This ratio is higher than an unleveraged [index fund's](/index-fund/) fee because of the embedded costs of swaps and futures. The fund trades during regular market hours with reasonable liquidity and typically tight [bid-ask spreads](/bid-ask-spread/), though trading volume can thin at times. Most investors can enter and exit efficiently without material slippage, provided they trade during peak hours and in reasonable size.

## Who should own ONDG, and for how long?

ONDG is designed for traders and tactical investors with a specific, near-term thesis about Ondas Holdings. A trader believing ONDS will rally 10 percent over the next five days might buy ONDG to capture a 20 percent move. Someone who thinks ONDS has bottomed after a sharp fall might use ONDG for a multi-day recovery bet. ONDG is explicitly not suitable for investors buying and holding for months or years. Advisors routinely warn against holding any leveraged ETF beyond a few days to a few weeks at most. The volatility decay and the high expense ratio combine to erode returns meaningfully over longer periods, turning what looked like a winning strategy into an expensive loss.

## How do traders research and monitor ONDG?

Start with the Leverage Shares prospectus and factsheet, which explain the daily reset mechanics and the expense ratio. Track the fund's daily performance against 2x the daily return of ONDS stock to spot tracking error. If ONDG consistently deviates materially from 2x the underlying move, that signals a problem worth investigating. Monitor Ondas Holdings itself—its price action, news, earnings releases, sector trends—since the entire investment thesis depends on understanding ONDS. Set a target holding period (days to two weeks maximum) at entry and exit when that target arrives rather than letting a short-term trade drift into a long-term mistake.
