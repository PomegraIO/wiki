**The fund.** TDTT holds [Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities](/tips/) concentrated in the 3-year maturity slice. Shorter duration than the 5-year version (TDTF). Maturities cluster around 2–4 years, tracking the iBoxx 3 Year TIPS Index managed by Markit. Issued and managed by Northern Trust Investments, FlexShares series, NASDAQ-listed.

**What "3-year duration" means.** Not the same as maturity. A [bond](/bond/) with 3-year maturity might have 2.8-year duration if its coupon is high enough. Duration measures interest-rate sensitivity: a 3-year duration bond drops about 3 percent in price for every 1 percentage point rise in yields. Shorter duration = less portfolio volatility when rates move, but also less upside when rates fall.

**The [inflation](/inflation/) hedge.** Like all TIPS, each bond's principal and coupon adjust with [Consumer Price Index](/consumer-price-index/) changes. If inflation runs 3 percent in a year, a TIPS that started with a $1,000 face value adjusts to $1,030; the [coupon payment](/coupon-payment/) also scales up proportionally. Owner receives the inflation adjustment whether holding to maturity or selling on the [secondary market](/secondary-market/). Short-duration TIPS mean the inflation adjustment compresses into a quicker time frame — the bond matures sooner and the principal is returned adjusted for inflation to date.

**Costs and liquidity.** [Expense ratio](/expense-ratio/) around 0.20 percent. [Bid-ask spreads](/bid-ask-spread/) are tight for a specialized bond fund, though volume is lighter than the broadest Treasury ETFs. Less friction for a buy-and-hold but price impact matters if you are trading large blocks.

**Where the risk sits.** TIPS eliminate [inflation risk](/inflation-risk/); they do not eliminate [interest-rate risk](/interest-rate-risk/). If the [Federal Reserve](/federal-reserve/) raises rates, the market value of the fund declines even though you collect inflation-adjusted coupons and recover principal at maturity. A 3-year duration means about half the interest-rate sensitivity of a 6-year bond or longer; still material, but blunted. [Real yields](/real-yield/) on 3-year TIPS are often pinned near zero or slightly negative, because the market expects inflation and prices those expectations into demand.

**Practical use cases.** A cash-alternative position for an investor worried inflation will erode savings. Part of a short-duration [bond ladder](/bond-ladder/). An inflation hedge for a portfolio that otherwise owns equities and longer nominal bonds. Not a vehicle for total return or yield generation. The point is purchasing-power preservation plus modest real return.

**Research compass.** Check the prospectus for the iBoxx index composition and methodology. Compare the real yield on 3-year TIPS to other short-duration options — savings accounts, money-market funds, short-dated [Treasury bonds](/treasury-bond/). Historical rolling returns versus nominal Treasuries show whether TIPS protection has been worth the trade-off. Watching [inflation expectations](/inflation-expectations/) and Fed policy gives color on whether TIPS valuations are attractive or wide at a given moment.
