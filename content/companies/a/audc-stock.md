*[**AUDIOCODES LTD**](/wiki/audc-stock/) (**AUDC**) is an Israeli-based [software](/wiki/software-industry/) company that develops communications and voice networking solutions for [enterprises](/wiki/enterprise-software/) and telecommunications service providers.*

## What the company does

AUDIOCODES develops cloud and on-premises software platforms for voice, video, and data communications. Its products serve two main segments: Communications Software (cloud-based unified communications and voice network solutions) and Products (hardware appliances and softswitches for carriers and enterprises). The company primarily targets telecommunications carriers, service providers, and large enterprises seeking to modernize legacy voice infrastructure or migrate to cloud-based systems.

The company's cloud-native offerings focus on voice quality management, session management, and interoperability in hybrid and cloud deployment scenarios. Its solutions often function as intermediary software layers that bridge legacy [PSTN](/wiki/public-switched-telephone-network/) networks with internet-based communications protocols.

## How it makes money

AUDIOCODES generates revenue through software licenses, cloud subscriptions, and maintenance and support contracts. The business model reflects a blend of on-premises licensing (one-time perpetual or term licenses) and recurring cloud subscription revenue. Professional services and implementation consulting round out the revenue base. The company derives meaningful geographic revenue from North America, Europe, and emerging markets, with a concentration in carriers and service providers that deploy its software at scale.

## Where it sits in its industry

The software voice networking space sits at the intersection of enterprise [telecommunications](/wiki/telecommunications-sector/) and cloud computing. AUDIOCODES competes with established infrastructure software vendors, cloud communications platforms, and custom engineering solutions. Its position emphasizes compatibility with diverse network architectures—a valuable niche as enterprises balance legacy voice systems with modern [VoIP](/wiki/voip-technology/) and cloud migration strategies.

The company's focus on carriers and service providers, rather than just end-user enterprises, gives it a different competitive footprint than enterprise-focused unified communications vendors. Carrier networks often require specialized voice quality and network management capabilities that AUDIOCODES targets.
