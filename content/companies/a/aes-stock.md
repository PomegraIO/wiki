*[**AES Corp**](https://pomegra.io/wiki/aes-stock/), trading under ticker **AES**, is a [public company](/wiki/public-company/) engaged in global electricity generation and distribution. Headquartered in Arlington, Virginia, AES operates power generation facilities and electrical distribution utilities across a geographically diverse portfolio spanning North America, South America, Asia, and Europe.*

## What the company does

AES operates as a global electricity company with two primary business segments: power generation and electricity distribution. On the generation side, AES owns and operates power plants using diverse fuel sources including natural gas, coal, nuclear, hydroelectric, wind, and solar technologies. On the distribution side, AES operates regulated electric utilities serving residential and commercial customers in multiple countries. The company's geographic diversification spans developed and emerging markets, exposing it to varied regulatory environments and customer bases.

## How it makes money

AES generates revenue from two primary sources: wholesale electricity sales from generation facilities to utilities and large customers, and retail electricity sales from its regulated distribution utilities. Generation revenue depends on contracted prices, spot market sales, fuel costs, and plant utilization. Distribution revenue comes from regulated rate structures approved by national or regional regulators, providing more stable and predictable cash flows than generation. Profitability depends on managing fuel costs, operating expenses, capital costs, and regulatory rate recovery.

## Business segments and geographic exposure

The generation and distribution segments operate under different business models. Distribution utilities typically operate as regulated monopolies with stable revenues but limited profit upside. Generation operations are more exposed to commodity price risk and wholesale market conditions. AES' geographic diversification provides exposure to emerging market growth but also introduces currency risk, political risk, and regulatory uncertainty. The company must manage complex international operations spanning developed and developing economies with different regulatory frameworks.

## Industry transformation and strategic positioning

The global energy industry is undergoing transformation toward renewable energy, distributed generation, and decarbonization. AES faces ongoing capital investment needs for renewable energy development, grid modernization, and infrastructure maintenance. The company's diversified generation portfolio includes significant coal-generation assets, which face long-term transition risk as markets move away from fossil fuels. AES must balance existing asset performance with investment in renewable and cleaner technologies.
