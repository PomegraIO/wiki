*[**AWARE INC /MA/**](/wiki/public-company/) ([**AWRE**](/wiki/stock/)) is a [**US-listed**](/wiki/stock-exchange/) software company specializing in biometric identification and authentication technologies, primarily developing facial recognition, iris recognition, and fingerprint matching systems.*

## What the company does

AWARE develops biometric software and platform solutions that enable organizations to identify, verify, and authenticate individuals based on their physiological characteristics. Its core technologies focus on facial recognition, iris recognition, and fingerprint identification. The company licenses its software to government agencies, law enforcement, border control authorities, financial institutions, and private sector enterprises seeking to integrate biometric capabilities into their systems.

## How it makes money

AWARE generates revenue through licensing its proprietary software algorithms and identity verification platforms. Its business model includes upfront licensing fees, ongoing software-as-a-service (SaaS) arrangements, and maintenance and support contracts. Government contracts and international implementations form significant portions of its customer base, reflecting strong demand for biometric solutions in identity verification and security applications.

## Where it sits in its industry

AWARE operates in the broader identity verification and cybersecurity software landscape. The company competes with other biometric software providers and identity management platforms, positioning itself as a specialist in facial and iris recognition technology. Its customer base spans government agencies with critical infrastructure needs and private sector organizations modernizing their authentication systems. The biometric identification market has experienced sustained growth driven by increasing security requirements and digital identity initiatives.

## Technology and market considerations

The company's competitive positioning depends on the accuracy and reliability of its algorithms, the breadth of its software integrations, and its ability to comply with evolving regulatory frameworks governing biometric data. Privacy regulations in jurisdictions worldwide have created both compliance requirements and market opportunities for identity verification solutions. The shift toward digital identity programs and remote authentication has expanded demand for robust biometric systems, though regulatory scrutiny around facial recognition technology remains a consideration for the industry.
