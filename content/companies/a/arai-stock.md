## What the company does

Arrive AI develops and commercializes autonomous driving technology, focusing on solutions for transportation and logistics applications. The company combines artificial intelligence, machine learning, and sensor systems to enable vehicles to operate with minimal human intervention.

## How it makes money

The company generates revenue through multiple channels: licensing autonomous vehicle technology to third parties, providing AI software and platform services, offering fleet management solutions, and potential direct deployment of autonomous vehicles. Licensing and subscription-based models form a core part of the commercial strategy.

## Where it sits in its industry

Arrive AI operates in the competitive autonomous vehicle technology space, competing alongside established automotive manufacturers, technology firms developing self-driving systems, and other autonomous vehicle startups. The focus on logistics and transportation applications positions the company within a broader ecosystem of companies working to modernize last-mile delivery and freight operations.
