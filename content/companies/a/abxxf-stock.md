*[Abaxx Technologies Inc.](#) (ABXXF) is a fintech and commodities trading company engaged in developing technology platforms and infrastructure for trading precious metals, commodities, and other assets. The company provides trading systems, clearing services, and market connectivity for commodity market participants.*

## What the company does

Abaxx Technologies operates in the commodities trading infrastructure space, providing electronic trading platforms, settlement systems, and connectivity services for participants in precious metals and commodity markets. The company may operate or manage trading venues, provide software and technology systems used by traders and brokers, or offer clearing and settlement services that facilitate commodity transactions.

## How it makes money

The company generates revenue through transaction fees charged on trades executed through its platforms, subscription fees for access to trading systems and market data, clearing and settlement fees, and technology licensing. Revenue scales with trading volume and the number of active participants using its platforms and services. The company may also earn revenue from other services such as market data distribution or risk management tools.

## Market opportunity and competitive position

Commodity trading markets are large and fragmented, spanning precious metals, agricultural products, energy, and other commodities. Abaxx competes with established commodity exchanges, alternative trading systems, technology vendors serving commodity markets, and traditional brokerage firms. The market is characterized by consolidation, with larger platforms achieving scale advantages in liquidity and cost competitiveness.

## Technology and infrastructure focus

Success depends on the reliability, speed, and functionality of trading platforms, the breadth of commodities accessible, integration with broader financial infrastructure, and the ability to attract traders and other participants. Regulatory compliance and security are critical requirements. The company must continuously invest in technology to remain competitive with larger, established market participants.
