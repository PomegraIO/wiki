## Platform and Technology

Abaxx has built a digital marketplace infrastructure designed to streamline how traders buy and sell physical commodities—metals, agricultural products, and other raw materials that typically move through opaque, fragmented channels. Rather than relying on traditional commodity exchanges and paper-based settlement, the platform uses blockchain-based systems to create a more transparent, faster path from transaction to settlement. The company's technology stack aims to modernize infrastructure that has remained largely unchanged for decades, reducing friction and settlement times for institutional traders and commodity producers.

## Market Positioning

The commodities markets are enormous but operationally antiquated. Settlement can take days, [price discovery](/price-discovery/) is uneven across geography, and operational costs accumulate for all participants. Abaxx occupies a niche at the intersection of commodity trading, fintech, and [distributed ledgers](/distributed-ledger/)—positioning itself to capture inefficiencies if digital commodity trading gains adoption among institutional users. Success would require building sufficient liquidity and network effects to move material transaction volume away from legacy systems and competing against both established exchanges and newer fintech platforms.
