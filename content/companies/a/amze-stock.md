*[AMAZE HOLDINGS, INC.](/wiki/amaze-holdings-inc/) (**AMZE**) is a [publicly-listed](/wiki/public-company/) technology and commerce platform that enables digital creators, influencers, and entrepreneurs to design, sell, and monetize products directly to their audiences. The company operates as an end-to-end creator commerce ecosystem, combining tools for product creation, merchandising, [e-commerce](/wiki/e-commerce-platform/), and managed services to empower individual creators at scale.*

## What the company does

AMAZE HOLDINGS operates a multi-layered creator commerce platform designed to eliminate friction between creators and commerce. The company provides a comprehensive suite of tools including product design and customization capabilities, e-commerce infrastructure, payment processing, fulfillment logistics, and audience engagement features. Creators can leverage the platform to design merchandise, apparel, digital goods, and other products; list them on the Amaze marketplace or their own branded stores; and reach audiences through integrated social commerce tools. The platform integrates with major social media networks to enable shoppable experiences directly within social feeds.

## How it makes money

The company generates revenue through multiple streams within its creator commerce ecosystem. Primary revenue sources include [marketplace commissions](/wiki/marketplace-commission/) on transactions conducted through the platform, fulfillment and logistics services fees, payment processing charges, and premium subscription tiers for creators seeking advanced features. The company also earns revenue from brand partnerships and collaborations that connect established companies with its creator network.

## Where it sits in its industry

AMAZE HOLDINGS competes within the broader creator economy infrastructure sector, which includes platforms enabling influencers and content creators to monetize their audiences. The company's competitive positioning centers on providing an all-in-one solution combining design, merchandising, fulfillment, and audience tools, rather than requiring creators to cobble together multiple disparate services. The platform serves millions of creator stores and operates a substantial network of food, lifestyle, art, and entertainment creators. Following the acquisition of The Food Channel in 2024, the company has expanded into culinary creator content and commerce, positioning itself to serve niche creator verticals with dedicated communities.

## Recent strategic developments

In 2022, AMAZE HOLDINGS (then operating under a different entity) acquired Spring, a mature creator merchandise platform with 5.5 million creator stores, folding the acquisition into its existing portfolio alongside legacy Teespring brand assets. This consolidation unified multiple creator commerce properties under one platform. In 2024, the company completed the acquisition of The Food Channel, a digital platform focused on culinary content, for $650,000 via convertible debt. This acquisition enabled Amaze to combine its food creator network with an established digital brand to build a next-generation social commerce and entertainment platform for the culinary space. The company has signaled intentions to achieve debt-free status and expand gross merchandise value and net revenue in subsequent periods.
