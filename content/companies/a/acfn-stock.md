## What the company does

ACORN Energy develops and commercializes energy technology solutions addressing power generation and energy efficiency needs. The company's product portfolio includes distributed generation systems, energy management technologies, and related products serving industrial operations, commercial facilities, and remote applications requiring reliable power supply.

Product development focuses on technologies improving energy efficiency, reducing operating costs, and enhancing reliability for customer operations. Solutions may be customized for specific customer applications or offered as standardized products deployable across multiple customer sites.
