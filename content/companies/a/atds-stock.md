*Data443 Risk Mitigation, Inc. (ATDS)* is a provider of enterprise data security and privacy management software. The company delivers solutions designed to help organizations identify, classify, protect, and manage sensitive data across a range of environments—from local devices to networks, cloud platforms, and databases. Operating across both on-premises and cloud settings, the company serves organizations seeking comprehensive approaches to data governance and threat prevention.

## What the company does

Data443 Risk Mitigation develops and delivers software platforms focused on data security and privacy. The company's portfolio includes solutions for ransomware recovery, data classification, access controls, privacy compliance, and threat intelligence. Its products are designed to operate across heterogeneous IT environments—covering devices, networks, cloud infrastructure, and databases. The company positions itself around protecting sensitive information regardless of where it resides or how it moves through an organization.

## Core product categories

The company's offerings span multiple security domains. Data identification and classification tools help organizations discover and categorize sensitive information. Access control and governance solutions manage permissions and enforce policies across cloud and on-premises systems. Ransomware recovery and incident response tools allow organizations to restore systems to operational states following attacks. Privacy compliance platforms help organizations meet regulatory obligations around data handling and consumer notification. Threat intelligence services provide monitoring and detection capabilities. The company also develops specialized tools for blockchain protection, data archiving, and secure content distribution.

## Market position and customer base

Data443 serves a global customer base operating across more than 100 countries. The company's addressable markets include enterprises managing large volumes of sensitive data, healthcare and financial services organizations subject to strict compliance regimes, and smaller organizations seeking consolidated data protection solutions. The company operates as a small business by SEC definition and is classified as an emerging growth company, positioning it within the competitive landscape of mid-market and enterprise cybersecurity vendors.

## Business model and acquisitions

The company generates revenue through software licensing, subscription services, and professional services related to implementation and integration. Data443 has pursued a growth strategy that includes acquiring complementary technology assets and companies. In recent years, the company has integrated acquisitions including threat intelligence platforms and ransomware protection technologies, broadening its product reach. The company also operates strategic partnerships with infrastructure providers to expand its operational capacity.

## Regulatory and compliance considerations

As a public company, Data443 files regular reports with the SEC including [10-K](/wiki/10-k/) annual filings and quarterly 10-Q forms, which detail its financial performance, risk factors, and operational developments. The company's business is subject to data protection regulations, cybersecurity compliance frameworks, and industry-specific requirements that shape product development and operational practices.
