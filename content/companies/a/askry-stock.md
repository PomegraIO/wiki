## What the company does

Asker Healthcare Group develops diagnostic imaging systems and related healthcare technology solutions. The company's product portfolio centers on advanced imaging equipment designed for use in hospital and clinical environments. Its systems serve medical professionals who require reliable diagnostic capabilities for patient care and treatment planning.

## How it makes money

The company generates revenue through the sale of diagnostic imaging equipment and related technologies to healthcare providers. Revenue streams include equipment sales, software licensing, and potentially service and maintenance contracts associated with its imaging systems. The business model follows traditional medical device revenue patterns, with recurring revenue opportunities through service agreements and software subscriptions.
