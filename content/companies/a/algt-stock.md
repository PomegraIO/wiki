*[Allegiant Travel CO](https://www.allegiantair.com/)* (**ALGT**) is a publicly traded [airline](/wiki/airlines-industry/) based in the United States, operating as an ultra-low-cost carrier (ULCC). The company specializes in serving leisure travelers on leisure routes, avoiding major hub-to-hub competition and focusing instead on point-to-point service from secondary markets to vacation destinations.

## What the company does

Allegiant operates a network of narrow-body jet aircraft serving leisure routes across the United States. Unlike traditional [full-service airlines](/wiki/full-service-airline/), Allegiant eschews complexity—no connecting flights, no hub infrastructure, and minimal frills. The airline focuses on price-sensitive leisure passengers traveling from smaller markets to vacation destinations: Las Vegas, Orlando, beaches, and ski resorts. This niche positioning reflects a business model inherited from its founding, when it began as a charter operator before transitioning to scheduled service.

## How it makes money

Allegiant's revenue model combines several income streams. Ticket revenue is the primary driver, but the company is notable for its reliance on ancillary fees: baggage charges, seat selection, boarding priority, and onboard concessions all constitute material revenue. This "unbundled" pricing approach allows the airline to advertise low base fares while capturing revenue from add-on services. The company also generates supplemental revenue through its relationship with its own destination resort properties, tour packages, and promotional tie-ins with leisure travel partners.

## How it compares in its industry

Allegiant occupies the extreme low-cost end of the US airline spectrum. While carriers like Southwest and Spirit Airlines also compete on cost, Allegiant uniquely targets the leisure segment and avoids major metropolitan hub competition entirely. This positioning insulates it from head-to-head competition with [United Airlines](/wiki/ual-stock/), [American Airlines](/wiki/aal-stock/), or [Delta Air Lines](/wiki/dal-stock/), which offer extensive networks and connecting service. Conversely, Allegiant has lower operational complexity than traditional carriers but faces tighter margins and higher sensitivity to fuel prices, economic downturns, and travel demand volatility. Its smaller scale and specialized route network also constrain growth compared to network airlines.

## Operational characteristics

The airline operates a relatively young, homogeneous fleet of Boeing 737 and Airbus A320 aircraft, keeping maintenance and crew training standardized. Flight times are kept short—typically under 4 hours—to minimize crew layovers and maximize aircraft utilization. Allegiant has notably high employee scheduling efficiency and low labor costs relative to competitors, though the airline faces ongoing labor relations challenges as staff seek compensation alignment with larger carriers. The company maintains minimal ground infrastructure, often using secondary airports with lower landing fees.
