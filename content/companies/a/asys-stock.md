*AMTECH SYSTEMS INC* (**ASYS**) is a designer and manufacturer of semiconductor manufacturing equipment, focused on single-wafer processing systems for advanced materials deposition and removal applications in the semiconductor industry.

## What the company does

AMTECH SYSTEMS develops and sells single-wafer processing systems used in semiconductor manufacturing, with a focus on atomic layer deposition (ALD), dielectric etch, and advanced materials processing. These systems are used by integrated circuit manufacturers and foundries to create patterns and deposit materials on silicon wafers during the fabrication process. The company's technology targets applications in logic, memory, and advanced packaging segments of the semiconductor industry.

## How it makes money

AMTECH's revenue comes primarily from the sale of capital equipment systems to semiconductor manufacturers, along with service contracts and spare parts. The company also generates revenue through product development contracts and support services. Equipment sales are typically high-dollar transactions dependent on semiconductor industry capital spending cycles. Revenue streams include direct equipment sales to major foundries and integrated device manufacturers, as well as aftermarket support.

## Where it sits in its industry

AMTECH operates in the [semiconductor equipment](/wiki/stock-exchange/) manufacturing segment, a highly specialized and capital-intensive sector serving the broader semiconductor industry. The company competes with larger, diversified equipment manufacturers and more focused specialists, facing competition based on technology performance, customer relationships, manufacturing capability, and pricing. Success depends on continued demand for advanced process nodes and the company's ability to innovate in single-wafer processing technologies.
