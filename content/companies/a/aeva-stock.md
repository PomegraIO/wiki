*Aeva Technologies, Inc., trading under the ticker **AEVA**, is a sensor technology company specializing in the development of 4D imaging sensors based on frequency-modulated continuous-wave (FMCW) lidar. The company operates in the rapidly evolving sector of autonomous vehicle perception systems and advanced industrial sensing.*

## Core technology

Aeva develops 4D imaging sensors using FMCW lidar technology. Unlike traditional lidar systems that generate 3D point clouds, FMCW lidar simultaneously captures depth, intensity, and velocity information in a single measurement, creating what the company describes as 4D perception. This approach aims to provide enhanced object detection and tracking capabilities compared to conventional 3D lidar.

## Target markets

The company targets two primary markets: autonomous vehicle manufacturers and industrial applications. In autonomous vehicles, perception systems—including lidar, radar, and cameras—are critical for the vehicle's ability to detect obstacles, identify lanes, and navigate safely. Industrial applications include robotics, infrastructure monitoring, and other sensing use cases where 3D spatial awareness is valuable.

## Competitive landscape

The lidar market includes established automotive suppliers like Velodyne, Innoviz, and Luminar, as well as major technology companies integrating in-house perception systems. Aeva's FMCW approach differentiates it technically but faces the challenge of building customer relationships in a market dominated by proven suppliers and integrated systems. Success depends on technological advantages being validated by customers and on achieving manufacturing scale and cost targets.

## Manufacturing and commercialization

Like most sensor manufacturers, Aeva must navigate the challenges of moving from prototype to production volumes, managing supply chain complexity, and achieving cost levels acceptable to automotive customers who operate on thin margins. The company has pursued strategic partnerships and customer collaborations to accelerate commercialization.
