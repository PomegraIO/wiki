## Catheter-Based Valve Innovation

Anteris Technologies Global is a structural heart medical device company developing transcatheter valve technology for patients with severe aortic stenosis. Unlike traditional surgical valve replacement, which requires open-heart surgery, DurAVR—the company's lead product—is delivered through a minimally invasive catheter-based approach. This positions patients for shorter hospital stays, faster recovery, and reduced procedural trauma while addressing a substantial and growing patient population. Anteris went public in December 2024, trading on [NASDAQ](/nasdaq/) and the ASX under ticker AVR.

The company's core innovation centers on valve biomechanics. DurAVR's engineering mimics the mechanical performance of a healthy human aortic valve, a design principle intended to address durability limitations seen in competing transcatheter valves over longer observation periods. Aortic stenosis affects millions globally, with demographic aging expanding the eligible patient pool. The company's origination in Australia combined with Minneapolis-based U.S. operations reflects both scientific heritage and market positioning for North American regulatory and commercial engagement.

## Clinical Path to Market

Anteris's commercialization roadmap hinges on the PARADIGM pivotal clinical trial, designed to generate regulatory and clinical evidence for FDA approval and global reimbursement. In January 2026, Medtronic—the world's largest independent medical technology company—invested $90 million strategically in Anteris, paired with an underwritten public equity offering that raised approximately $320 million in total gross proceeds. Medtronic's participation signals confidence in the technology and establishes a framework for potential manufacturing, distribution, and commercial partnership as DurAVR moves toward approval.

As a pre-revenue clinical-stage device company, Anteris's investor profile resembles development-stage medtech: capital efficiency, trial [execution risk](/execution-risk/), and regulatory pathway success are central to value creation. The competitive landscape includes established valve manufacturers with entrenched clinical relationships. Success depends on DurAVR clearing regulatory hurdles, demonstrating superior clinical outcomes, and capturing meaningful market share in the transcatheter aortic valve replacement space. Investors track trial enrollment rates, regulatory feedback, and Medtronic relationship developments as key inflection points.
