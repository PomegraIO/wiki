## What the company does

Abundia Global Impact Group develops and manages initiatives promoting sustainability and social impact. The company may operate projects directly or partner with other organizations. Business models vary by initiative—some projects generate recurring revenue through operations, while others may involve project development, consulting, or equity stakes in portfolio companies.

## Impact investing and social enterprise model

The company operates at the intersection of financial returns and social/environmental impact. This dual mandate creates both opportunities and challenges—impact projects may generate lower financial returns than purely commercial ventures but create social or environmental value. Funding comes from impact investors, development finance institutions, philanthropic grants, and commercial financing. The company must demonstrate both financial viability and measurable impact to attract capital.

Abundia likely generates revenue from operating sustainable projects, management fees from portfolio companies or initiatives, consulting services, or equity returns from portfolio investments.
