*Grupo Aval Acciones Y Valores S.A.* (**AVAL**) is a Colombian financial services holding company and one of the largest financial conglomerates in Latin America, offering integrated banking, insurance, and investment management services through multiple subsidiaries and affiliates operating across the region.

## What the company does

Grupo Aval serves as a financial holding company anchoring a conglomerate of banking, insurance, and capital markets subsidiaries. Its primary business segments include personal and commercial banking, insurance underwriting, pension fund management, asset management, and securities brokerage. The company operates through major subsidiaries including Banco de Bogotá, Banco del Occidente, Banco Sudamerica, and Banco Av Villas, with significant insurance operations through Seguros Monterrey New York Life and other entities. This structure allows Grupo Aval to offer integrated financial services across multiple countries in Latin America, capturing both traditional banking relationships and capital market transactions.

## How it makes money

Grupo Aval generates revenue primarily from net interest income derived from its banking operations, insurance premiums from underwriting activities, fees and commissions on investment and brokerage services, and gains on securities trading and asset management. The company earns spreads on lending portfolios across personal, commercial, and corporate segments, while insurance subsidiaries generate underwriting income and investment returns on premium reserves. Commission-based revenue comes from securities brokerage, asset management, and various advisory services. Pension fund management and retirement products create recurring fee income tied to assets under administration.

## Market position and strategy

Grupo Aval ranks among the largest financial conglomerates in Latin America by assets and market capitalization, with significant scale advantages in Colombian financial services and growing regional presence. The company benefits from its diversified operating model—spreading risk across banking, insurance, and capital markets rather than relying on a single revenue stream. Its subsidiary structure, while adding complexity, allows it to serve different customer segments and market niches. Aval has pursued regional expansion and deepened its presence in wealth management and asset administration, positioning itself to capture higher-margin fee-generating businesses alongside traditional lending. The holding company format enables operational flexibility and allows each subsidiary to maintain distinct brand equity while sharing back-office infrastructure and cross-selling opportunities.

## Where it fits in its industry

In Colombian financial services, Grupo Aval is one of the top-tier players competing against other major banking groups. Regionally, it competes with other large Latin American financial conglomerates and international banks with Colombian operations. Unlike many pure-play banks, Aval's holding company structure and diversified subsidiaries position it similarly to integrated regional financial groups—broader in scope but more complex to evaluate than single-business banks. Its strength in insurance and asset management differentiates it from traditional retail-focused banks, while its Colombian and regional foundations distinguish it from global money-center banks.
