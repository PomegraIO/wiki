*[**Axil Brands, Inc.**](/wiki/public-company/) (**AXIL**) is a [US-listed](/wiki/stock-exchange/) specialty manufacturer operating in hearing protection, audio enhancement, and personal care markets. The company designs and distributes audio and hearing products alongside hair and skin care solutions, serving both tactical professionals and mainstream consumers across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific regions.*

## What the company does

Axil Brands manufactures and distributes two distinct product lines. Its primary segment covers hearing protection and audio enhancement devices—ear plugs, earmuffs, ear buds, and outdoor speakers branded under AXIL. These products serve the sporting goods, recreational, military, federal agent, law enforcement, tactical, and industrial sectors. The company's secondary segment comprises hair and skin care treatments, including shampoos, conditioners, scalp treatments, and styling solutions marketed under the Reviv3 Procare brand.

## How it reaches customers

The company employs a diversified distribution strategy spanning direct-to-consumer e-commerce, third-party online marketplaces, wholesale distributors, and salon partnerships. This multi-channel approach allows Axil to reach tactical professionals, outdoor enthusiasts, industrial workers, and salon-based beauty consumers simultaneously. The geographic footprint extends across the United States, Canada, Europe, Australia, New Zealand, Africa, and Asia.

## Market positioning

Axil operates in the specialist protective equipment space, which overlaps with broader consumer discretionary and personal care categories. The hearing protection market serves both occupational safety mandates and recreational niches (firearms, motorsports, music). The personal care line diversifies revenue and spreads customer acquisition costs. This dual-segment structure differs from single-category consumer companies, creating exposure to both industrial and cosmetic demand cycles.

## Company history

Axil Brands traces its public history through a corporate restructuring. The company was previously known as Reviv3 Procare Company before changing its name to Axil Brands, Inc. in February 2024. This rebrand reflected a strategic pivot to emphasize the hearing protection business, which had become the dominant revenue contributor and market differentiator.
