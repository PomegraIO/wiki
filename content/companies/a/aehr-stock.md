*Aehr Test Systems Inc. (ticker AEHR) is a semiconductor equipment manufacturer specializing in wafer test systems and thermal processing equipment used in chip manufacturing. The company designs and manufactures systems that test semiconductor wafers for defects and functionality before they are cut into individual chips. Aehr's products serve semiconductor fabs (manufacturing facilities) and chipmakers worldwide.*

## What the company does

Aehr Test Systems manufactures wafer probe test equipment and thermal processing systems used in semiconductor manufacturing. Wafer testing equipment applies electrical signals to semiconductor wafers and measures responses, identifying defective chips before they are cut and packaged. Thermal processing equipment subjects wafers to temperature cycles, simulating operating conditions to stress-test chips. These systems are critical quality assurance tools in semiconductor production, helping manufacturers improve yields and reduce defects.

The company also provides test programs and software customization services, supporting deployment of its equipment in customer fabrication facilities. Equipment is typically sold directly to semiconductor manufacturers through sales and application engineering teams.

## How it makes money

Aehr generates revenue through equipment sales. Systems are complex, engineered products with significant upfront development and manufacturing costs. Customers purchase test equipment to increase fab throughput, improve yield, or meet new testing requirements for next-generation chips. Equipment pricing reflects engineering complexity, customer customization, and market competition. Installation, integration, and training services provide additional revenue. Maintenance contracts and spare parts sales create recurring revenue from the installed base.

Profitability is driven by manufacturing efficiency, overhead absorption, and the company's ability to command premium pricing for innovative test solutions that improve yields or reduce cost-of-test.

## Where it sits in its industry

Aehr operates in the semiconductor equipment segment, competing against larger, diversified equipment manufacturers (KLA, Applied Materials, ASML, Teradyne) and specialized test equipment vendors. The semiconductor equipment industry is highly specialized, with vendors often focusing on specific process steps or equipment types. Aehr's focus on test and thermal processing creates specialization advantages but limits addressable market relative to broad-platform equipment makers.

Demand is cyclical, tied to semiconductor industry capital spending, which correlates with chip demand, fab utilization rates, and availability of capital. Semiconductor manufacturing is highly capital-intensive; fab operators make lumpy, large capital investments during expansion phases. Test equipment upgrades occur as new process nodes require updated testing.
