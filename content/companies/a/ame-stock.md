## What the company does

AMETEK manufactures electronic instruments, electromechanical devices, and related technologies. Its portfolio spans analytical and monitoring instruments, power and process management systems, and equipment for industrial automation. The company serves customers in [aerospace](/wiki/aerospace-industry/), defense, power generation, oil and gas, semiconductor processing, and industrial automation sectors. Operations span manufacturing facilities and distribution networks across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific regions.

## How it makes money

The company generates revenue through two primary business segments: Electronic Instruments and Electromechanical Solutions. Electronic Instruments includes analytical instruments, test and measurement equipment, and process management systems sold to industrial and aerospace customers. Electromechanical Solutions encompasses specialty connectors, actuators, and motion control systems. Revenue comes from direct sales to original equipment manufacturers (OEMs), system integrators, and aftermarket customers across distributed global channels. Recurring revenue streams include service contracts and replacement parts.

## Where it sits in its industry

AMETEK competes in the diversified industrial equipment manufacturing sector alongside larger conglomerates and specialized instrument makers. The company positions itself on technical depth, reliability, and customer relationships rather than lowest-cost competition. Its dual focus on both measurement/control systems and physical components gives it exposure to multiple industrial end markets. The company has historically grown through [organic expansion](/wiki/organic-growth/) and strategic acquisitions of complementary instrument and device makers.
