*[Addtech AB/ADR](/wiki/addtf-stock/) is a **Swedish industrial technology company** that distributes and sells technical products, components, and solutions to engineering, manufacturing, and construction industries. The company operates as a [public corporation](/wiki/public-company/) and is traded in the U.S. through [American Depository Receipts (ADR)](/wiki/adr/).*

## What the company does

Addtech operates as a distributor and supplier of industrial and technical products to engineering and construction sectors. The company sources, distributes, and sells a diverse portfolio of technical components, equipment, and solutions used in manufacturing, construction, and infrastructure projects. Its business model involves acting as a supply chain intermediary, connecting manufacturers and suppliers with end customers across European and international markets.

## How it makes money

Addtech generates revenue through product sales and distribution services to industrial and engineering customers. The company earns margins through purchasing products from manufacturers and suppliers, then reselling to end customers at higher prices. Revenue streams include direct product sales, specialized supply solutions, and technical services. Profitability depends on sales volume, supplier relationships, pricing power, and operational efficiency.

## Where it sits in its industry

Addtech operates in the fragmented industrial distribution sector, competing against larger multinational distributors, regional distribution companies, and direct manufacturer sales channels. The company differentiates itself through product selection, customer relationships, supply chain expertise, and geographic presence in Northern Europe and other markets. Success depends on maintaining supplier relationships, customer loyalty, and efficient supply chain operations.
