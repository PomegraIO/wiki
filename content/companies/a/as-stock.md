*[**Amer Sports, Inc.**](/wiki/public-company/) (**ticker: AS**) is a multinational manufacturer and marketer of athletic equipment and apparel, operating through a portfolio of heritage brands focused on sports, recreation, and consumer products. The company sits in the [consumer discretionary](/wiki/consumer-discretionary/) sector and derives revenue from equipment, apparel, and related merchandise for athletic activities.*

## What the company does

Amer Sports manufactures and sells sporting goods and athletic equipment across diverse categories. Its portfolio encompasses brands that serve athletes, recreational users, and consumers across multiple price tiers and demographics. The company's product lines span team sports (balls, equipment), individual sports gear, athletic apparel, and consumer recreation products. Distribution spans professional, collegiate, retail, and direct-to-consumer channels, with significant geographic presence across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific.

## How it makes money

The company generates revenue through the sale of branded sporting equipment, apparel, and related consumer products. Margins vary by product category and distribution channel—premium direct-to-consumer and professional/collegiate channels typically carry higher margins than mass retail. The company also earns from licensing agreements and partnerships with teams and athletes. Cost of goods sold reflects raw material inputs (rubber, textiles, synthetics), manufacturing labor, and supply chain logistics. Operating leverage improves at higher volumes, though the business remains sensitive to discretionary consumer spending and seasonal demand patterns.

## Market position and competitors

Amer Sports competes in the broad athletic equipment and apparel market alongside multinational [conglomerates](/wiki/conglomerate-discount/) like Nike and Adidas, as well as specialized equipment makers and regional players. The company's heritage and brand recognition in specific categories—particularly team sports equipment—provide defensible positions in those segments. However, the overall market is competitive and subject to shifts in consumer preferences, retail consolidation, and pressure from direct-to-consumer models. Vertical integration with some brands and acquisition of complementary product lines have been strategies to expand category coverage and distribution reach.

## Capital structure and ownership

The company operates as a public corporation with shares listed on a major US exchange. Like most mature consumer goods manufacturers, it carries debt from acquisitions and operations. Management and incentive structures typically emphasize [ROIC](/wiki/return-on-invested-capital/), [free cash flow](/wiki/cash-flow-statement/), and [return on equity](/wiki/return-on-equity/). The company manages working capital across inventory, receivables, and payables as part of normal business operations.
