## What the company does

ADC Therapeutics develops antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) therapies that combine the targeting specificity of monoclonal antibodies with potent anti-cancer drugs. The company's approach leverages ADC technology to deliver cytotoxic payloads directly to cancer cells while minimizing systemic toxicity. Its pipeline includes therapeutic candidates in clinical development targeting lymphomas, leukemias, and solid tumors.

## How it makes money

As a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company, ADC Therapeutics does not yet generate substantial product revenues. The company finances operations through equity financing, research collaborations, and potentially partnership agreements with larger pharmaceutical companies. Milestone payments and licensing deals may provide interim funding sources. Long-term revenue depends on successful clinical development, regulatory approval, and commercialization of ADC therapies.

## Where it sits in its industry

ADC Therapeutics competes in the specialized antibody-drug conjugate and cancer immunotherapy sector, competing against larger pharmaceutical companies, established biotech firms, and other specialized cancer therapy developers. The company differentiates itself through its ADC technology platform and clinical pipeline. Success depends on demonstrating clinical efficacy advantages and advancing candidates efficiently through development phases.
