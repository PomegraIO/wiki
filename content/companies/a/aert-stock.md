*[**Aeries Technology, Inc.**](https://pomegra.io/wiki/aert-stock/), trading under ticker **AERT**, is a [public company](/wiki/public-company/) operating in educational technology. Headquartered in the United States, Aeries Technology develops and operates cloud-based software platforms serving K-12 school districts and educational institutions, providing student information systems and school management solutions.*

## What the company does

Aeries Technology develops and operates cloud-based software platforms serving K-12 public and private schools. The company's core product is a comprehensive student information system (SIS) that school districts use to manage student records, enrollment, attendance, grades, scheduling, and administrative workflows. The platform serves as a central hub for school operations, integrating data related to student performance, financial management, and operational efficiency. Schools access Aeries software through a subscription model with cloud hosting and ongoing technical support.

## How it makes money

Aeries generates recurring subscription revenue from school districts and private schools using its cloud-based software platform. Revenue is primarily driven by the number of users or by per-student or per-district pricing models. The company also generates ancillary revenue from implementation services, training, customization, and support services. As a SaaS business, Aeries' profitability depends on customer acquisition, customer retention, and expansion within installed customer base through additional module adoption and price increases.

## Market position and growth drivers

Aeries operates in the education technology market, competing with established student information system vendors and other EdTech platforms. The company's market depends on adoption of cloud-based solutions by school districts transitioning from legacy systems or seeking advanced functionality. Growth drivers include digital transformation of K-12 education, demand for data-driven decision making, and schools seeking to improve operational efficiency. The company benefits from the large installed base of schools still using older systems.

## Business model and customer relationships

Aeries operates a subscription SaaS model with relatively high customer switching costs given the centrality of student information systems to school operations. Customers typically require multi-year contracts, creating stable recurring revenue. The company must manage customer satisfaction, system performance, and feature development to maximize retention and expansion. Aeries must also navigate changing education policy, data privacy regulations, and integration requirements with other school software systems.
