*[Atkore Inc.](/wiki/public-company/) (**ATKR**) is a global manufacturer of electrical and mechanical products, primarily serving construction and infrastructure markets with a focus on electrical raceway systems, conduit, tubing, and specialty products.*

## What the company does

Atkore manufactures a broad range of electrical and mechanical products essential to construction, infrastructure, and industrial applications. Its core product lines include electrical raceway systems (metal conduit, PVC conduit, cable tray), mechanical tubing, protective cables, and closure systems. The company serves electrical contractors, builders, distributors, and end-users across residential, commercial, industrial, and utility construction segments. Atkore's products are used in building wiring, HVAC systems, telecommunications infrastructure, and data center installations.

## How it makes money

Atkore generates revenue through the manufacture and sale of standardized and engineered electrical and mechanical products, primarily to distributors and directly to contractors and construction firms. The business model relies on consistent demand for building materials and infrastructure components across economic cycles. Pricing is tied to commodity input costs (particularly steel and copper), which are passed through to customers via periodic price adjustments. The company operates manufacturing facilities across North America and internationally, optimizing production for regional demand patterns and supply chain efficiency.

## Business segments and geography

The company organizes operations around its product categories: electrical raceway and protective systems, mechanical products, and specialty solutions. Geographic exposure spans North America (a significant portion of revenue), with additional operations and distribution in other regions. Atkore benefits from exposure to both new construction activity and maintenance/replacement demand from existing building stock.

## Where it sits in its industry

Atkore competes in the electrical and mechanical products manufacturing space alongside larger diversified industrial conglomerates and smaller specialized manufacturers. The industry is characterized by standardized products, price competition based on commodity input costs, and the importance of distribution networks and contractor relationships. Atkore's position as a focused pure-play manufacturer in electrical raceway and mechanical tubing gives it specialized scale and expertise, though it faces cyclical demand tied to construction spending and building permit activity.
