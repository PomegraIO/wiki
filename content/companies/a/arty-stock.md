The iShares Future AI & Tech ETF (ticker: ARTY) is a thematic exchange-traded fund that gives investors exposure to companies building and deploying artificial intelligence systems, alongside broader technology innovation. Issued by BlackRock's iShares division, it tracks a custom index constructed to capture the intersection of AI and technology, aiming to offer a focused bet on companies benefiting from the AI cycle — from chipmakers and cloud providers supplying the computational backbone, to software firms building applications on top of it.

## The AI and technology components

The fund organizes itself around two broad tiers of the artificial intelligence economy. The infrastructure layer includes semiconductor designers and manufacturers supplying the chips that power AI training and inference — graphics processing units, custom AI accelerators, and the foundries that produce them. The compute and cloud layer includes major cloud providers and data center operators that host AI workloads and offer API access to AI services. These companies earn revenue from customers spinning up AI models in their facilities and paying for usage.

The application layer — software firms, enterprise platforms, and businesses embedding AI into their core products — forms the final segment tracked by ARTY. This includes companies building large language models and AI assistants, as well as enterprises across sectors that deploy AI internally to improve operations or customer-facing features. The fund's theme hinges on the idea that all three layers will expand together over time: more chips sold means more cloud capacity deployed, and more infrastructure attracts more software innovation.

## How the fund works and its costs

ARTY is a traditional ETF, not a leveraged or inverse product, so it moves in line with the performance of its underlying holdings without daily reset mechanics or decay risk. The fund trades on a major [stock exchange](/stock-exchange/), meaning investors buy and sell shares at market prices throughout the day, just as they would a single stock. The spread between the bid and ask prices is typically narrow for ARTY, given BlackRock's scale and the fund's popularity, so trading costs for investors are modest.

The annual [expense ratio](/expense-ratio/) — the fund's published fee expressed as a percentage of assets under management — is quoted in basis points and is economical relative to actively managed technology funds, though it reflects the active selection and rebalancing involved in constructing a thematic index rather than tracking a free-float weighted market index passively. The fund rebalances periodically, usually on a quarterly or semi-annual schedule, to maintain alignment with its AI and technology mandate.

## Concentration and sector risk

Because ARTY focuses deliberately on artificial intelligence and technology, it carries [concentration risk](/concentration-risk/). The fund is not diversified across the full market the way a broad-based equity [index fund](/index-fund/) is; rather, it picks a subset of companies believed to benefit from AI expansion. Technology stocks, particularly those dependent on cyclical capital spending and semiconductor demand, are themselves volatile. During periods of technology sector weakness — rising [interest rates](/interest-rate/), diminished expectations for AI spending, or profit-taking after rapid gains — ARTY will likely underperform the broader market substantially.

The thematic selection also creates the risk of category drift. The boundary between "AI-exposed" and "general technology" is porous and subjective; different managers or indices draw it differently, and the fund's holdings may shift as the AI opportunity evolves or as other technology trends emerge.

## Who the fund serves and how to research it

ARTY is designed for investors who believe artificial intelligence and the companies supplying it will outperform the broader market, and who are willing to accept sector concentration and volatility to pursue that view. It suits investors with a multi-year horizon who understand that technology cycles bring booms and busts; it is not appropriate for those seeking portfolio stability or neutral market exposure.

Prospective investors should consult the fund's prospectus and fact sheet from iShares, which detail the exact composition, the index methodology, and the fee structure. The prospectus includes the fund's investment objectives and risk disclosures, and the fact sheet provides a current snapshot of top holdings, sector composition, and other key statistics. Tracking the fund's largest positions and sector weights gives a clear sense of the AI ecosystem as the fund manager sees it; when major holdings shift or the sector composition changes materially, that often signals how the fund is interpreting AI's evolution.
