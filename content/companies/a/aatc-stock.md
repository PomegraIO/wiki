*[**AUTOSCOPE TECHNOLOGIES CORP**](/wiki/aatc-stock) (**AATC**) is a publicly traded industrial technology company specializing in advanced imaging and vision systems. The company operates in the technology and transportation sector, developing computer vision and imaging solutions for traffic monitoring, transportation infrastructure, and related applications.*

## Product portfolio and technology

AUTOSCOPE develops and manufactures computer vision and imaging systems designed for traffic monitoring, congestion detection, incident detection, and transportation management. The company's products employ advanced imaging sensors, video processing algorithms, and software analytics to gather and interpret transportation data. These systems provide data to traffic management agencies, transportation departments, and infrastructure operators.

## Market applications and customers

The company serves transportation departments, municipal governments, traffic management agencies, and private transportation infrastructure operators. Core applications include traffic flow monitoring, incident detection, congestion management, and data collection for transportation planning. Customer bases are typically government agencies and infrastructure operators managing highway systems, urban streets, and public transportation networks.

## Business model and revenue

AUTOSCOPE generates revenue through product sales, installation and integration services, software licensing, and ongoing maintenance and support contracts. The business model depends on selling hardware devices with accompanying software, along with recurring revenue from support and software services. Like technology companies serving infrastructure markets, success depends on product performance, customer acquisition, and building recurring revenue streams.
