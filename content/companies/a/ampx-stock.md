*[Amprius Technologies, Inc.](/wiki/public-company/) (AMPX) is a semiconductor and battery manufacturer specializing in advanced [lithium-ion batteries](/wiki/lithium-ion-battery/) based on nanowire technology, serving the consumer electronics and energy storage sectors.*

## What the company does

Amprius designs and manufactures lithium-ion batteries using proprietary nanowire anode technology. Rather than conventional graphite anodes, the company employs silicon nanowire structures to increase energy density and performance. This approach targets applications where compact, high-capacity power storage is critical—primarily smartphones, tablets, wearables, and fixed grid energy storage systems. The nanowire architecture allows for more lithium ions to be stored per unit volume, yielding batteries with higher energy density than traditional designs.

## How it makes money

Amprius generates revenue through manufacturing and licensing agreements. The company operates production facilities and sells batteries directly to [original equipment manufacturers (OEMs)](/wiki/original-equipment-manufacturer/) in consumer electronics. Licensing arrangements allow other manufacturers to produce batteries incorporating Amprius technology under patent agreements. Revenue is sensitive to semiconductor production cycles, consumer device demand, and competition from incumbent battery makers.

## Technology and competitive positioning

The nanowire battery approach addresses a long-standing trade-off in lithium-ion chemistry: energy density versus cycle life and safety. Amprius's silicon nanowires are engineered to withstand the structural stresses that typically degrade silicon anodes in conventional batteries, reducing the brittleness problems that have historically limited silicon use. The company holds numerous patents covering nanowire materials, coating strategies, and anode architecture.

Competition comes from established battery manufacturers—[Panasonic](/wiki/panasonic-stock/), [Samsung](/wiki/samsung-stock/), and [CATL](/wiki/catl-stock/)—who have invested heavily in conventional and newer lithium chemistries. Smaller innovators pursuing solid-state and alternative anode approaches represent emerging competitive threats.

## Where it sits in its industry

Amprius operates at the intersection of semiconductor and battery manufacturing, a niche where raw materials, equipment supply chains, and fabrication expertise overlap. The company is smaller than the dominant global battery producers but holds technological differentiation through nanowire patents and manufacturing know-how. Its business model relies on technology licensing and selective direct manufacturing, rather than full-scale production at the scale of Tesla's Gigafactory or CATL's facilities.
