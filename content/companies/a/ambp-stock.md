*<strong>Ardagh Metal Packaging S.A.</strong> (<strong>AMBP</strong>) is a global manufacturer of aluminum and tinplate metal packaging, primarily for beverage and food containers. The company operates production facilities across North America, Europe, and other regions, serving major consumer goods and beverage companies worldwide as a critical infrastructure provider in the packaging supply chain.*

## What the company does

Ardagh Metal Packaging manufactures aluminum cans, steel cans, and other metal containers for beverages (beer, soft drinks, energy drinks) and food products. The company supplies major multinational beverage and food producers, making its products a fundamental component of consumer goods distribution. Production is highly automated and scale-intensive, requiring significant capital investment in furnaces, casting systems, and assembly lines.

## How it makes money

Revenue comes primarily from selling metal containers by volume, typically under long-term supply agreements with major beverage and food companies. Pricing is influenced by underlying commodity costs (aluminum and steel), with contracts often including pass-through mechanisms for raw material fluctuations. The company also generates revenue from specialty products and services. Profitability depends on manufacturing efficiency, plant utilization rates, and cost management relative to commodity input prices.

## Where it sits in its industry

The metal beverage can industry is consolidated, with a small number of large producers serving global beverage makers. Ardagh competes with other major can manufacturers on cost, production capacity, geographic reach, and reliability. The sector has structural advantages related to beverage consumption growth, sustainability trends favoring aluminum's recyclability, and high switching costs for customers. Competitive position is tied to operational efficiency, access to raw materials, and the ability to serve customers at scale across multiple regions.

## Environmental and regulatory context

Metal packaging, particularly aluminum cans, benefits from strong recycling infrastructure and environmental positioning. The industry operates within regulations governing emissions, waste, and workplace safety. Aluminum production and recycling are energy-intensive, making energy costs and grid composition material factors in profitability and competitive positioning.
