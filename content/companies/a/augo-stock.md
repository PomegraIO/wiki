*[Aura Minerals Inc.](https://www.auraminerals.com/) (**AUGO**) is a precious and base metals mining company operating gold and copper mines in Brazil and Mexico. The company develops and extracts ore deposits through open-pit and underground mining operations, selling its products to global commodity markets.*

## What the company does

Aura Minerals is engaged in the exploration, development, and mining of precious metal deposits in Brazil and copper-gold properties in Mexico. The company operates mines that produce gold, copper, and silver as primary outputs, with the metals extracted through conventional mining methods suited to each deposit's geology and depth. The company maintains a portfolio of properties in different stages of development, from established producing mines generating near-term cash flow to exploration-stage assets with longer development horizons.

## How it makes money

Aura's revenue comes from the sale of refined metal concentrates (for base metals) and bullion (for gold and silver) into commodity markets where prices are set by global supply-demand dynamics. Operating leverage exists in the relationship between mining costs (which vary with labor, energy, and grade) and commodity prices. The company focuses on mines with lower average mining costs relative to industry peers, allowing profitable operations across commodity price cycles. Capital expenditure to expand production or move deposits into production represents a significant use of cash flow.

## Where it sits in its industry

Aura operates in the small-to-mid cap segment of the gold and copper mining sector, below large multinational miners by market capitalization and asset scale. The company competes with regional operators and larger diversified miners for access to quality deposits, skilled labor, and capital funding. Geographic focus on Latin America—a region with established mining infrastructure and regulatory frameworks—positions the company within a competitive set of miners operating in the Western Hemisphere.
