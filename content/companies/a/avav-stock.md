*[AeroVironment Inc](https://www.aerovironment.com) (**AVAV**) is a [public company](/wiki/public-company/) that designs, manufactures, and supports unmanned aircraft systems, loitering munitions, and related technologies for defense, government, and commercial markets. Based in Arlington, Virginia, the company serves U.S. military branches, allied governments, and commercial enterprises with both small-scale tactical platforms and larger systems for intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance.*

## What the company does

AeroVironment develops and produces unmanned aircraft systems (UAS) and related technologies across two primary business segments. The company's portfolio includes hand-launched quadcopters for tactical military operations, medium-range systems for extended surveillance missions, and loitering munitions (suicide drones) designed for precision targeting. Its platforms are engineered for rapid deployment in field conditions, with emphasis on durability, ease of use, and real-time data transmission.

The company also provides related services including training, logistics support, and field integration for military and government customers. Many of its systems emphasize portability and rapid setup, allowing small units to conduct reconnaissance and strike operations with minimal infrastructure.

## How it makes money

Revenue flows primarily from government and defense contracts with the [U.S. Department of Defense](/wiki/department-of-defense/), international allied governments, and defense prime contractors who integrate AeroVironment systems into larger platforms. The company generates income through direct product sales, multi-year supply agreements, and service contracts for maintenance and training.

A growing commercial segment addresses civilian applications including precision agriculture, infrastructure inspection, and public safety. Commercial customers purchase systems outright or subscribe to data services derived from aerial observations. Government contracts typically involve fixed-price development programs, production orders, and sustainment agreements over multiple years.

## Strategic position in the industry

AeroVironment operates in the specialized segment of small-to-medium unmanned systems, competing with established defense contractors and emerging technology firms. The company's competitive advantages rest on decades of experience in battery and electric propulsion technology, proven reliability in austere environments, and deep integration with U.S. military doctrine and procurement processes.

The broader unmanned systems market has experienced substantial growth due to increased military spending on modernization, allied nations' acquisition of U.S. defense technology, and expanding use of drones in border security and infrastructure applications. AeroVironment's position reflects decades of product iterations and relationships within the defense establishment, though the company faces competition from larger defense contractors diversifying into autonomous systems and emerging international manufacturers.

Investors should examine the concentration of government revenue, changes in customer mix, and backlog trends relative to production capacity. Track contract wins announced in press releases, which often signal future revenue. Cross-reference major customer names and contract values against [SEC](/wiki/sec/) filing disclosures to understand revenue concentration risk. Review the company's technology roadmap and product development timelines relative to military modernization priorities.
