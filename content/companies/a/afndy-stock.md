*Allfunds Group PLC., listed on US exchanges as an American Depository Receipt (ADR) under the ticker **AFNDY**, is an investment platform and financial technology company serving the wealth management and investment fund distribution ecosystem. The company operates infrastructure connecting institutional investors, fund managers, and financial advisors.*

## Investment platform infrastructure

Allfunds operates digital platforms that facilitate interactions between fund managers, wealth management firms, financial advisors, and end investors. The platform streamlines fund distribution, enables digital onboarding, provides portfolio management tools, and supports transaction settlement. By digitizing and centralizing these functions, Allfunds improves operational efficiency across the investment ecosystem.

## Fund distribution services

A core function of Allfunds' platform is fund distribution—the connection between fund managers seeking investor capital and distributors (banks, advisors, wealth managers) seeking to offer funds to clients. Traditionally, this has been a fragmented, manual process. Allfunds' platform enables direct connections, simplifies fund selection and purchase, and provides real-time data on fund performance and availability.

## Wealth management enablement

The company serves wealth management firms and financial advisors by providing technology that supports their operations: client onboarding, portfolio management, compliance reporting, and transaction execution. This enables smaller wealth managers to compete more effectively by reducing operational complexity and allowing them to focus on client service and advisory relationships.

## Market position and customers

Allfunds serves a diverse customer base across Europe and other regions, including wealth managers, banks, insurance companies, pension funds, and fund managers. The company's business model benefits from network effects: as more participants join the platform, its value increases for all members. Revenue is generated through transaction fees, subscription fees for platform access, and data services.

## Regulatory and operational environment

Financial technology platforms must navigate complex regulatory requirements including anti-money laundering, know-your-customer procedures, and transaction reporting rules. Allfunds operates across multiple jurisdictions, each with its own regulatory framework. Compliance infrastructure and regulatory relationships are important operational requirements.
