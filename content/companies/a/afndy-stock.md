Allfunds Group is a British B2B software and services platform that sits between asset managers and the financial advisors, banks, and institutions that distribute their funds to end clients. Rather than being a consumer-facing wealth manager itself, Allfunds operates the plumbing: it operates technology platforms, carries out [fund accounting](/fund-accounting/) and ManCo services, and provides data solutions that help banks and advisers buy, sell, and manage investment products more efficiently. The company serves traditional wealth channels—private banks, retail banks, insurance firms, pension funds, [custodians](/custodian/)—as well as independent financial advisers who need industrial-grade fund operations and distribution infrastructure.

The platform's economics rest on recurring subscription and transaction fees from participants who rely on Allfunds' core portal (Allfunds Connect) and ancillary services for fund administration, regulatory compliance, and portfolio analytics. The company has also invested in blockchain infrastructure through Allfunds Blockchain, positioning itself in the longer-term transition of fund settlement and post-trade operations. Its reach spans the UK and continental Europe, with depth in supporting the complex regulatory and operational demands of institutional and intermediate wealth distribution in developed markets.
