**Angel Oak [Mortgage REIT](/mortgage-reit/) originates and holds non-qualified mortgages—loans that fall outside traditional lending guardrails—and runs a [securitization](/securitization/) operation.**

The company functions as a mortgage originator and balance-sheet lender rolled into one. Its main business is sourcing non-qualified mortgage loans (those that don't meet [Fannie Mae](/fannie-mae/) or [Freddie Mac](/freddie-mac/) standards), originating them through correspondent networks, and then either securitizing the loans into mortgage-backed securities or holding them in its portfolio. It retains [credit risk](/credit-risk/) on the loans it holds, which is where the REIT income comes from.

## The origination-to-securities machine

Angel Oak's operating model sits at the intersection of mortgage lending and structured products. It originates non-QM loans—often to borrowers with stated income, recent [credit events](/credit-event-sovereign/), or non-traditional employment—and either keeps the loan on its books or packages it into a securitization for sale to investors. The securitization arm generates fees and accelerates capital turnover, while the portfolio of retained mortgages generates interest income and provides the equity cushion for securities issued against them.

## Exposure to residential real estate and credit cycles

As a mortgage REIT holding residential credit risk, Angel Oak is levered to refinance rates, borrower payment behavior, and overall housing stability. When rates rise and loans perform, the portfolio holds value; when rates fall sharply or credit tightens, losses can appear. The non-QM segment carries higher credit risk than Agency-backed mortgages but historically commands higher yields. [Interest rate](/interest-rate/) [hedging](/wiki/basis-risk/) and swap arrangements are part of standard REIT treasury management, though the extent varies by market conditions.

## At a glance

- Originates and invests in non-qualified residential mortgages
- Operates securitization platform for capital-efficient loan distribution
- Earns interest on portfolio holdings and fees on originated loans
- Exposed to borrower [prepayment risk](/prepayment-risk/), credit losses, and interest-rate margin compression
- Operates as a mortgage REIT under tax-advantaged structure
