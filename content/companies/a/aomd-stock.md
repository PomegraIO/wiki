*[**Angel Oak Mortgage REIT, Inc.**](/wiki/public-company/) (**AOMD**) is a [mortgage real estate investment trust](/wiki/stock/) that specializes in acquiring and managing residential mortgage-backed securities ([RMBS](/wiki/stock-exchange/)) and non-performing mortgage loans. The company operates within the mortgage finance sector, originating value through the purchase and management of mortgage assets.*

## What the company does

Angel Oak Mortgage REIT acquires and holds portfolios of residential mortgage-backed securities and non-performing mortgage loans. The company's strategy centers on identifying undervalued mortgage assets and managing them to realize returns through principal recovery, interest payments, and portfolio appreciation. Non-performing loans represent mortgages in default or delinquent status, which Angel Oak works to resolve through loan modification, refinancing, or asset disposition.

## How it makes money

The REIT generates revenue through multiple channels. Interest income flows from mortgage-backed securities held in its portfolio and from non-performing loans that are rehabilitated and brought current. The company also realizes gains from the sale of managed assets when market conditions permit profitable exits. Management of non-performing loans may yield returns through loan workouts, where borrowers cure delinquencies and resume payments. As a mortgage REIT, Angel Oak distributes a portion of its taxable income to shareholders in the form of regular dividends, a structural requirement for REITs under US tax law.

## Where it sits in its industry

Angel Oak competes in the mortgage REIT sector, a specialized corner of real estate finance focused on residential mortgage assets rather than property ownership. Mortgage REITs differ fundamentally from traditional equity REITs, which own physical real estate. Angel Oak's focus on non-performing loans and discounted RMBS positions it in the distressed and value-oriented segment of mortgage finance, requiring specialized management expertise and loss mitigation capabilities. The sector's performance is sensitive to interest rate environments, mortgage delinquency rates, and housing market conditions.

## Dividend and structure

As a mortgage REIT, Angel Oak is required to distribute at least ninety percent of taxable income annually to shareholders. The company's capital structure relies on both equity funding and leverage, which amplifies returns and risks in a low-rate environment. Mortgage REITs are typically managed for yield, with distributions forming a material portion of total return alongside potential price appreciation or depreciation of the underlying asset portfolio.

## How to research it

Quarterly [10-Q](/wiki/10-k/) filings and annual [10-K](/wiki/10-k/) filings with the SEC (CIK 1766478) contain detailed information on portfolio composition, asset quality metrics, leverage ratios, and management commentary. These regulatory documents provide visibility into the proportion of RMBS versus non-performing loans, delinquency trends, and recovery rates. Investor presentations and earnings call transcripts often explain the company's acquisition strategy and economic outlook. Mortgage REIT research should track prevailing mortgage rates, mortgage origination volumes, and default/delinquency rates as leading indicators of portfolio performance.
