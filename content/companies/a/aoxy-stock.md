*[Advanced Oxygen Technologies Inc](https://sec.report/CIK/352991) (**AOXY**) is a publicly traded manufacturer of oxygen generation and compression systems for medical, industrial, and aerospace markets. The company designs and produces equipment that separates and concentrates oxygen from ambient air, serving hospitals, clinics, industrial facilities, and specialized applications in aviation and spacecraft environments.*

## What the company does

Advanced Oxygen Technologies manufactures oxygen concentrators, compressors, and related gas handling equipment. The company's core product line focuses on oxygen generation systems that extract and purify oxygen from air without requiring external gas supplies. These machines serve medical institutions, including hospitals and clinical settings where reliable oxygen supply is essential, as well as industrial facilities requiring oxygen for manufacturing processes, metal cutting, and chemical applications.

The company also serves aerospace and specialty markets where compact, efficient oxygen generation or pressurization systems are required. Advanced Oxygen's equipment emphasizes reliability and consistent performance, particularly in environments where supply chain disruptions or access to bulk oxygen delivery present operational challenges.

## How it makes money

The company generates revenue primarily through equipment sales, including the manufacturing and distribution of oxygen concentrators and compressor units. Revenue streams include direct sales to healthcare facilities, industrial clients, and equipment distributors who integrate the systems into their offerings.
