*[AGREE REALTY CORP](/wiki/adc-stock/) is a **real estate investment trust (REIT)** that owns and operates a diversified portfolio of retail and commercial properties leased to tenants across the United States. The company operates as a [public corporation](/wiki/public-company/) in the real estate sector.*

## What the company does

Agree Realty owns and manages a diversified portfolio of retail and commercial real estate properties across the United States. The company operates primarily as a net-lease REIT, where tenants bear responsibility for most operating expenses and property maintenance. The property portfolio includes shopping centers, office properties, and individual retail locations leased to national and regional retailers and service businesses.

## How it makes money

As a REIT, Agree Realty generates revenue primarily through rental income from tenant leases. The company collects base rent and, in many cases, additional percentage rents and recovery of operating expenses. Under the REIT structure, the company distributes a substantial portion of taxable income to shareholders as [dividends](/wiki/dividend/). Revenue stability depends on tenant credit quality, lease terms, occupancy rates, and rent collection.

## Where it sits in its industry

Agree Realty operates in the retail real estate sector, competing against other retail REITs, private real estate investors, and traditional commercial real estate companies. The company differentiates itself through portfolio composition, tenant diversification, lease structures, and geographic spread. Success depends on acquiring quality properties at favorable valuations, maintaining high occupancy rates, and managing tenant relationships effectively.

## How to research it

Investors researching Agree Realty can examine its [10-K](/wiki/10-k/) and [10-Q](/wiki/10-k/) SEC filings to understand property portfolio composition, tenant concentration, lease terms, occupancy rates, and capital structure. REIT-focused research provides insights into sector dynamics, interest rate sensitivity, and property valuations. Real estate market reports and retail property analysis offer context for evaluating the company's portfolio quality and growth prospects.
