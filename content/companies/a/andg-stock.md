*[Andersen Group Inc.](/wiki/public-company/) (**ANDG**) is a [construction services](/wiki/public-company/) company providing design-build solutions across structural systems, heavy industrial, and commercial sectors. Based in the United States, the firm combines architectural design, engineering, and construction expertise to deliver integrated projects for clients in diverse industries.*

## What the company does

Andersen Group provides full-service construction and engineering solutions. The firm operates as a design-build contractor, meaning it combines design and construction under a single entity rather than separating those functions. This integrated approach simplifies project management and can reduce timelines and costs for clients. The company works on structural systems projects, heavy industrial facilities, and commercial buildings—sectors that require technical expertise in engineering and complex logistics.

## How it makes money

Revenue comes from construction contracts where Andersen Group is hired to design and build projects on a fee or fixed-price basis. Like most construction firms, the company earns income by winning bids, managing project delivery, and controlling costs against contract terms. The design-build model creates multiple value propositions: clients may reduce project duration and risk by working with a single entity accountable for both design quality and construction execution. Margins depend on project complexity, competition for bids, and operational efficiency.

## Where it sits in its industry

The construction and design-build sector includes regional and national players of varying scale. Andersen Group competes in the mid-market segment, focusing on specialized sectors rather than general contracting across all segments. The design-build model has gained market share in recent decades as clients recognize potential cost and schedule benefits. Competition in the industry is fragmented and price-sensitive, with differentiation driven by technical capability, safety track records, reputation, and relationships with large industrial clients.
