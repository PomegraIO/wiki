Agassi Sports Entertainment Corp., trading under the ticker AASP with SEC CIK 930245, sits at the intersection of sports, entertainment, and gaming hospitality. The company's operations have centered on the management and operation of sports facilities, entertainment venues, and gaming-related entertainment properties. Its core business model involves leasing or owning facilities that host events—ranging from professional matches to entertainment shows—and capturing revenue from ticket sales, concessions, and ancillary services tied to those venues.

The company's main product lines historically included sports venue management, gaming entertainment facilities, and event promotion. These operations generate income through multiple channels: direct admission fees, food and beverage sales, gaming operations at partner venues, and licensing arrangements with sports properties. The mix of gaming and sports entertainment differentiates it from pure sports-management firms, as the company can monetize both the entertainment draw and the gaming activity within its properties.

Like many entertainment and hospitality operators, Agassi faces structural challenges tied to venue capacity constraints, labor costs, and regional economic cycles. The sports-entertainment sector requires sustained capital investment in facilities and heavy marketing to maintain traffic, and profitability hinges on consistent attendance and high-margin ancillary spending. The company's position in gaming-adjacent hospitality means it benefits when [discretionary spending](/discretionary-spending/) rises but becomes vulnerable during consumer retrenchment.

As of recent activity, Agassi remains a publicly traded micro-cap with modest liquidity.
