## What the company does

ASTROTECH provides space-related technology and services spanning satellite ground operations, orbital services, and engineering solutions. The company operates throughout the aerospace supply chain, supporting both government agencies and commercial space businesses with specialized systems and expertise.

## How it makes money

The company generates revenue through contracts with government agencies and commercial space operators. Primary revenue streams include satellite-related services, engineering work, and technology solutions that support space operations and launch infrastructure.

## Where it sits in its industry

ASTROTECH competes in the broader aerospace and defense services sector, where larger primes and specialized suppliers serve the government and commercial space markets. The company focuses on niche capabilities in satellite operations and ground infrastructure, positioning itself in a market segment supported by sustained government spending and growing commercial space activity.

## Business structure and operations

The company's operations are organized around its core business units serving different customer segments in government and commercial space. These divisions handle contract fulfillment, engineering delivery, and ongoing customer support across multiple programs.
