**Adapti is a Nevada-based company repositioning itself at the intersection of sports representation, influencer management, and AI-powered brand matching.** Incorporated in 2007 and operating on OTC markets, the company formerly traded as Scepter Holdings before a 2025 rebrand signaled its strategic pivot away from purely consumer products.

For years, Adapti maintained a presence in health and beauty retail through its Dermacia product line, selling direct-to-consumer and via strategic retail partners. The business operated modestly in that space—selling existing brands online and exploring [acquisitions](/acquisition/) in the beauty segment. That model shifted dramatically starting in mid-2025. In July, Adapti acquired the Ballengee Group, a full-service sports agency representing Major League Baseball players. Months later, in April 2026, the company completed its acquisition of Levelution Sports Agency, LLC, establishing a multi-pronged platform spanning athlete representation and broader influencer management.

The company's stated strategy relies on proprietary AI and data analytics to match products and brands with influencers it deems optimal—a hypothesis that better algorithmic fit drives measurably higher marketing returns. Whether that edge materializes depends on execution; the space is crowded with talent agencies, and the OTC venue signals limited institutional confidence so far.

Adapti's filing status and public reports remain thin relative to larger agencies, but the company's recent sprint of acquisitions suggests conviction in the model. Success will hinge on whether its AI-driven matching algorithm delivers measurable ROI for brand partners and whether the sports representation vertical can be integrated profitably alongside influencer work. As an OTC stock, the company faces typical liquidity and disclosure constraints.
