**Anfield Energy pursues uranium and vanadium production through its owned and contracted processing and exploration assets.** The company operates in a sector that has gained momentum as nuclear power demand shapes energy policy globally. Anfield holds the Shootaring Canyon Mill—a fully permitted uranium processing facility in Utah—alongside uranium and vanadium development properties including its Velvet-Wood and Slick Rock projects. Its geographic footprint spans the western United States and Canada, positioning it within accessible mining jurisdictions while maintaining proximity to existing infrastructure and processing capacity that remains scarce across the industry.

The path to production distinguishes Anfield from pure exploration names. Rather than prospecting in greenfield territory, the company is developing near-term mining opportunities on established claims and employs a contracted mill asset that substantially de-risks the manufacturing side of uranium and vanadium recovery. This model reflects the physics and economics of the uranium cycle: milling capacity is finite and valued, particularly as fuel demand recovers through reactor refueling and new construction. Anfield's strategy centers on feeding the Shootaring Canyon Mill with ore from its own projects, creating a vertically integrated flow from extraction to product.

Funding and partnership structures matter heavily in this subsector. Anfield has arranged credit facilities through Extract Advisors to support capital requirements, a typical arrangement for developers navigating commodity cycles and project development phases. The vanadium and uranium markets remain volatile but are structurally supported by nuclear reactor refueling cycles and, increasingly, grid-scale battery demand for vanadium redox systems and energy storage applications. Investors tracking the nuclear renaissance and energy security themes typically monitor companies like Anfield for asset quality and execution progress rather than immediate profitability, given the development stage of the enterprise.

The company trades on the [NASDAQ](/nasdaq/), TSXV, and Frankfurt exchanges, reflecting investor distribution across North American and European markets. Scale relative to major uranium producers remains modest, placing Anfield in the development tier where execution risk and commodity leverage amplify returns. Attention tracks production milestones: permitting completions, ore development progress at its projects, mill utilization rates, and capital deployment efficiency as timelines toward production approach.
