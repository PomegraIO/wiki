ANTERO RESOURCES Corporation is an independent oil and natural gas exploration, development, and production company based in Denver, Colorado. The company operates primarily in the Appalachian Basin, one of North America's most prolific and stable natural gas regions, where it holds approximately 537,000 net acres. In addition to its core Appalachian properties, ANTERO maintains acreage in the Upper Devonian Shale and operates gathering and compression infrastructure comprising more than 700 miles of gas pipelines in the region. The company was incorporated in 2002 and restructured as a pure-play E&P company focused on Appalachian development and natural gas liquids extraction.

ANTERO's business model centers on three interconnected functions: upstream exploration and production of natural gas, oil, and NGLs; midstream gathering and marketing operations; and equity method investments in its affiliated Antero Midstream entity. This vertical integration allows the company to capture value across the value chain while maintaining operational flexibility in a volatile commodity market. The company's strategy emphasizes disciplined capital allocation, natural decline management, and leveraging its extensive gathering infrastructure to optimize production economics.

| Business Segment | Focus Area | Key Economics |
|---|---|---|
| **Exploration & Production** | Natural gas and NGL development in Appalachian Basin | Core margin generation; reserves replacement |
| **Gathering & Compression** | Midstream infrastructure (735+ miles of pipelines) | Fee-based revenue; operational leverage |
| **Midstream Investment** | Equity stake in Antero Midstream Corporation | Diversified revenue; cash distributions |

The company's operational foundation rests on established, long-lived reserves in a mature, well-understood geological setting. Unlike frontier exploration plays that depend on successful new discoveries, ANTERO's focus on core Appalachian resources allows predictable reserve depletion management and steady-state production optimization. The natural gas and NGL markets provide exposure to both energy commodities and petrochemical feedstocks, diversifying exposure relative to pure crude oil producers. The Appalachian Basin's geographic proximity to major U.S. industrial and utility demand centers offers logistical advantages and stable offtake agreements.
