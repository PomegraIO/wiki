*Ainos, Inc. (AIMD) is a biotech and AI-driven therapeutics company developing dual-platform innovations in smelltech sensing and oral interferon-based immune treatments. The company operates at the intersection of artificial intelligence and immunology, creating machine-readable smell identification technology alongside clinical-stage therapeutic candidates.*

## What the company does

Ainos operates two primary business platforms. Its smelltech division develops artificial intelligence systems that digitize scent into machine-readable data formats, branded as Smell ID. This AI-powered olfactory sensing technology processes molecular signatures into standardized data that can be deployed in industrial robotics, smart manufacturing, and diagnostic applications. The technology bridges the gap between biological olfactory perception and computational systems.

The company's therapeutic platform centers on VELDONA, an oral formulation of interferon. This low-dose systemic interferon candidate targets immune-mediated and infectious disease indications, with active clinical development in areas including primary autoimmune conditions and viral-related complications.

## How it makes money

The company's revenue generation occurs across two channels. The therapeutic segment derives value from advancing clinical-stage immunological candidates, with potential milestone and licensing agreements contributing income as programs progress through development phases. The smelltech platform generates revenue through technology licensing and deployment agreements with industrial and diagnostic partners seeking AI-powered olfactory sensing capabilities. Development stage companies typically rely on partnerships, grants, and milestone payments while advancing clinical pipelines.

## Where it sits in its industry

Ainos occupies a niche position within biotech that combines therapeutic development with emerging AI-sensing technology. Most therapeutic biotech companies focus on single-platform drug development; Ainos distinguishes itself through dual-platform diversification. In the interferon therapeutic space, the company targets underserved immune-mediated conditions with oral formulations, competing against larger pharmaceutical pipelines. In the smelltech segment, Ainos operates in an emerging category with limited direct competitors, as industrial olfactory AI remains largely in early commercialization stages. The company's size and stage position it as a specialized developer rather than a broad-market healthcare player.
