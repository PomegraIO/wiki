**ACCESS Newswire is a communications infrastructure company serving the disclosure and investor relations needs of public corporations.** Founded in the 1990s, the firm operates a network that helps companies distribute breaking news to investors, media outlets, and regulatory bodies—work that sits at the intersection of corporate governance and financial information flow.

The company's core offering is a distribution platform. When a public company needs to announce earnings, a merger, management changes, or regulatory filings, ACCESS runs the announcement through its network to reach stock exchanges, financial data terminals, news services, and the SEC's EDGAR system. This is unglamorous but essential plumbing in U.S. capital markets. A company cannot properly disclose material events without reaching the right ears at the right moment, and regulations require proof of that distribution.

Beyond raw distribution, ACCESS also offers supplementary services around investor relations—search engine optimization for investor sites, analytics on who reads the releases, and integration with the broader digital footprint of corporate announcements. The business benefits from a simple fact: public companies have no choice but to disclose, and they need reliable infrastructure to do it. The firm's revenue model is primarily subscription-based—tiers for different volumes and reach—plus à la carte fees for premium distribution.

The company is a long-standing player in a consolidated vertical. It competes with larger rivals like Business Wire and PRNewswire, but occupies a distinct niche serving mid-market and smaller public companies that value a more affordable, straightforward service. For decades, newswire distribution was a commodity; today it is a compliance and reputational necessity, which underpins stable recurring demand.

**Main products:**
- Press release distribution networks reaching journalists, exchanges, and financial terminals
- Investor relations site optimization and content hosting
- Distribution analytics and media pickup reporting
- [10-K filing](/wiki/10-k/) and regulatory announcement distribution
- Targeted media outreach to relevant journalists and outlets
