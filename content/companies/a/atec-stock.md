*[Alphatec Holdings, Inc.](/wiki/public-company/) (ATEC) is a medical device manufacturer focused on the global spine surgery market. The company designs, develops, and commercializes implants, surgical instruments, and biologic materials for spinal fusion and reconstruction procedures.*

## What the company does

Alphatec manufactures a broad portfolio of spine surgery solutions designed for both minimally invasive and open surgical approaches. The company's core offerings include spinal implants (such as interbody devices, fusion hardware, and reconstruction systems), surgical instruments tailored for spine procedures, and biologics used to promote bone fusion and tissue integration. These products address a range of indications, from degenerative disc disease to trauma and deformity correction.

## How it makes money

The company generates revenue primarily through the sale of spine surgery devices and implants to hospitals, surgical centers, and specialist practices. Revenue streams are organized around product categories and geographic markets. Alphatec sells directly through its own sales force in major markets and through distributors in international regions. Pricing in the medical device sector typically reflects the complexity of the implant, the surgeon's preference, and hospital procurement relationships.

## Where it sits in its industry

Spine surgery is a significant segment of the broader orthopedic and neuromusculoskeletal market. Major competitors include large diversified medical device companies as well as pure-play spine specialists. Alphatec competes on product innovation, ease of use for surgeons, clinical outcomes, and pricing. The spine market has long-standing incumbents, but innovation in minimally invasive techniques and patient outcomes continues to drive competitive positioning.

## Research and development

The company invests in research and development to advance its product pipeline, improve existing implant designs, and explore new biologics and surgical techniques. Innovation remains critical in spine surgery, where surgeons adopt new technologies that demonstrably improve patient recovery and reduce complications. Alphatec regularly publishes clinical data through peer-reviewed journals and surgical societies.
