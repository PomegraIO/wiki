*[Strive, Inc.](/wiki/strive-inc/) (ticker **ASST**) is a passive investment manager and financial services firm that specializes in creating and managing index-based exchange-traded funds and other investment products for institutional and retail investors.*

## What the company does

Strive, Inc. operates as a passive investment management firm, building and distributing a range of investment products—primarily exchange-traded funds—designed to track broad market indices. The company targets both individual investors and institutional clients with low-cost, transparent index-based strategies. Its product suite includes equity, fixed-income, and diversified allocation funds.

## How it makes money

Strive generates revenue primarily through management fees charged on assets under management in its investment funds. Like other asset managers, it collects a percentage of invested capital, with fees varying by product type and investor segment. The firm benefits from scale as assets grow, providing potential margin expansion. Additional revenue may come from advisory fees, custody arrangements, and ancillary investment services.

## Where it sits in its industry

The passive investing and index fund space is dominated by large, established players with massive asset bases. Strive competes in a market where cost leadership and product breadth are critical competitive advantages. The firm operates in a commoditized segment of asset management where differentiation often depends on fee structure, fund selection, distribution reach, and investor education. As a relative newcomer to the space, Strive must build recognition and scale against well-entrenched competitors.
