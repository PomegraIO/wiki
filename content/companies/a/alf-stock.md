Centurion [Acquisition](/acquisition/) Corp. ([NASDAQ](/nasdaq/): ALF) is a special purpose acquisition company incorporated in 2024 and based in New York. Like other SPACs, it is a blank-check company created to raise capital and merge with or acquire an operating business. Centurion closed its [initial public offering](/initial-public-offering/) in June 2024, raising $287.5 million, and is actively searching for a target acquisition in the technology sector.

## The SPAC structure and Centurion's capital raise

Centurion raised $287.5 million through the sale of 28,750,000 units at $10 per unit, with an additional 3,750,000 units issued when the underwriters exercised their over-allotment option. Each unit consists of one Class A ordinary share and half of one redeemable warrant, with whole warrants exercisable at $11.50 per share.

The capital sits in trust, earning interest, until Centurion identifies and completes a [business combination](/business-combination-purchase/). Unlike traditional equity offerings, SPAC investors have a redemption right — if they disagree with a proposed merger or acquisition, they can ask the company to return their capital at trust value. This mechanism creates accountability, as sponsors must convince a majority of shareholders to vote for any transaction.

## Investment focus: technology, gaming, and enabling infrastructure

Centurion's stated investment thesis is focused on technology and technology-enabled businesses across several verticals. The prospectus highlights:

**Interactive entertainment and gaming.** Video gaming, online entertainment platforms, and gaming-adjacent software and services rank among the primary targets. The gaming and interactive entertainment sector has grown into a multi-hundred-billion-dollar industry, and Centurion sees consolidation and technological innovation as ongoing drivers.

**Cybersecurity and AI.** As companies and governments grapple with digital security threats and artificial intelligence adoption, cybersecurity platforms and AI-powered infrastructure solutions represent a secondary focus area.

**Software as a Service (SaaS).** Business software, cloud-based applications, and subscription-model technology solutions are potential targets if they serve underserved markets or possess defensible technology moats.

**Deep tech.** Emerging hardware or software innovations — quantum computing, advanced semiconductors, novel materials science, or specialized infrastructure technologies — may also appeal to Centurion's mandate, provided they offer growth potential and a path to profitability.

## Why these sectors and what investors believe

Centurion's focus reflects a conviction that technology, gaming, and digital infrastructure will continue to attract capital and consolidate. A successful acquisition in one of these areas would give Centurion shareholders exposure to a growth-stage company, often with recurring revenue models (particularly in SaaS) and scalable operating leverage. Many of the companies Centurion might target are founders-led, profitable or approaching profitability, but seeking capital or a liquidity event for early shareholders.

## Timeline and execution risks

Centurion must complete a business combination within a specified period — typically two to three years — or face [liquidation](/liquidation/). That timeline creates pressure both to move quickly and to ensure a quality acquisition. Many SPACs have collapsed when sponsors struggled to find suitable targets at acceptable valuations, or when proposed [mergers](/merger/) fell apart due to shareholder votes or regulatory objections.

If Centurion fails to complete a transaction, its cash and warrants are returned to shareholders, and the SPAC dissolves. The net result is not necessarily negative for investors — they receive their capital back plus a modest amount of interest — but the opportunity cost may be significant if alternative investments would have outperformed.

## How investors and analysts assess Centurion

The primary sources of information are SEC filings, particularly 8-K forms announcing material developments or merger announcements, and the company's investor relations website. Once a target is announced, the proxy statement filed with the SEC provides detailed financial and operational information about the proposed acquisition, including management's projections and risk factors. That document is critical for shareholders to evaluate whether to vote in favor of the transaction.
