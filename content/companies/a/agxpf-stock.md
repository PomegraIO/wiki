*[**SILVER X MINING CORP.**](/wiki/agxpf-stock/) (ticker **AGXPF**) is a mineral exploration and development company focused on advancing precious metal and polymetallic projects, primarily in Peru. The company operates at the exploration and development stage, with a portfolio of properties targeting silver, copper, and associated metals.*

## What the company does

Silver X Mining is engaged in the acquisition, exploration, and development of mineral properties with emphasis on silver and copper mineralization. The company's project portfolio is concentrated in South America, particularly Peru, where it has staked claims and conducts field work to define mineral resources. As a junior exploration company, it operates at a pre-production stage, focusing on geological surveys, drilling programs, and metallurgical studies to assess the economic viability of its properties.

## Geographic and asset focus

The company's primary assets are located in Peru, a jurisdiction with established mining infrastructure and a long history of precious metal production. Peruvian properties offer exposure to prolific silver and polymetallic districts. Silver X pursues both direct ownership of claims and joint venture arrangements with other explorers and developers, allowing it to pursue multiple properties with limited capital.

## Capital structure and financing

As an exploration-stage company, Silver X operates with relatively modest resources compared to major miners. Funding comes primarily through equity issuances, option and warrant exercises, and periodic capital raises from investors. The company has limited operating cash flow and relies on periodic financing to fund exploration budgets. Share structure includes common stock and, at various points, preferred shares or warrants issued to raise capital and incentivize management and directors.

## How investors assess mining explorers

Mineral exploration companies are evaluated differently from producing miners. Key considerations include:

- **Property quality**: Geological merit of the projects, historical exploration data, and peer-reviewed assessments of mineralization potential
- **Management expertise**: Track record of the exploration team in discovering or advancing mineral deposits
- **Capital efficiency**: How effectively the company deploys shareholder capital to advance projects toward resource definition
- **Funding and runway**: Ability to finance exploration without excessive dilution; [cash burn rate](/wiki/cash-flow-statement/) and cash position
- **Regulatory environment**: Permitting pathway, environmental compliance, and community relationships in operating jurisdictions
