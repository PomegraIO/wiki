*[**Anjoy Foods Group Co., Ltd.**](/wiki/adr/) (**ANJFY**) is a Chinese food manufacturer and distributor specializing in snack foods and beverages, with its American Depositary Receipt listed on [OTC](/wiki/otc-pink-sheets/) markets.*

## What the company does

Anjoy Foods Group manufactures and distributes a range of packaged snack foods and ready-to-drink beverages. The company develops products for the Chinese consumer market, focusing on convenient snack formats that appeal to regional taste preferences. Its operations span food production, quality control, and distribution through retail channels.

## How it makes money

The company generates revenue from the sale of packaged snack foods and beverages to retailers and distributors within China. Revenue depends on product demand, retail placement, production capacity, and competitive positioning in the packaged foods segment. Operating margins reflect commodity input costs, manufacturing efficiency, and distribution logistics.

## Where it sits in its industry

Anjoy competes within China's packaged foods and snacks market against both multinational food corporations and established Chinese food manufacturers. The snack foods category is characterized by fragmented competition, evolving consumer preferences, and the need for effective distribution networks. As a smaller player, Anjoy differentiates through regional brand recognition and product variety tailored to local tastes.

## How to research it

Financial and operational details appear in [10-K](/wiki/10-k/) and 10-Q filings with the SEC under CIK 2098397. Annual reports describe product lines, geographic exposure, competitive conditions, regulatory compliance, and management strategy. Investor presentations, if available, may provide updates on market growth, cost structure, and capital allocation priorities.
