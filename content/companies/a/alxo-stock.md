*[ALX Oncology Holdings Inc](/wiki/public-company/) (ALXO) is a [clinical-stage biopharmaceutical](/wiki/pharmaceutical-company/) company focused on developing novel [immunotherapies](/wiki/immunotherapy/) for cancer treatment. The company's research and development efforts target both solid tumors and hematologic malignancies through innovative approaches to checkpoint and cell surface biology.*

## What the company does

Alx Oncology Holdings develops immunotherapeutic medicines designed to work across multiple therapeutic modalities. The company's pipeline includes drug candidates targeting mechanisms relevant to cancer treatment, with a focus on enhancing immune activation while mitigating immune suppression in the tumor microenvironment. The organization combines expertise in molecular biology, oncology, and immunology to advance novel treatment approaches.

## Research and clinical strategy

The company's development strategy centers on creating therapies that address fundamental mechanisms in cancer immunity. Rather than relying on a single therapeutic approach, Alx Oncology's pipeline spans both checkpoint modulation and cell surface biology strategies. This diversified approach allows the organization to explore multiple potential pathways for achieving anti-tumor activity while working to improve tolerability compared to existing treatment options.

## Business model and funding

As a clinical-stage company, Alx Oncology has not generated meaningful revenue from product sales. Instead, the company relies on capital markets and other funding sources to support its research and development operations. Like other companies in the biopharmaceutical sector at this stage of development, Alx Oncology's financial position depends on its ability to advance clinical programs, achieve regulatory milestones, and potentially establish partnerships with larger pharmaceutical organizations.
