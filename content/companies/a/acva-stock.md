## What the company does

ACV Auctions operates an online wholesale vehicle auction platform that facilitates transactions between dealers, commercial fleet operators, and other buyers of used vehicles. The platform leverages digital technology, including remote bidding and vehicle inspection tools, to streamline the wholesale vehicle auction process. The company's infrastructure includes logistics support and title management services to enable efficient wholesale transactions.

## How it makes money

The company generates revenue through transaction fees and commissions on vehicle sales conducted through its platform. Revenue per transaction typically reflects a percentage of the sale price or a fixed commission structure. Additional revenue streams may include subscription services for dealers, premium membership tiers, and ancillary services such as inspection, transportation, and documentation support.

## Where it sits in its industry

ACV Auctions operates in the fragmented wholesale used vehicle market, competing against traditional in-person auction houses, regional marketplaces, and other digital platform competitors. The company differentiates itself through technology, scale, user experience, and operational efficiency. Its platform model creates network effects as more buyers and sellers participate, increasing liquidity and transaction value.
