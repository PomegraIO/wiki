## The Micro-Cap Media Play

AI Era Corp operates as a small media and intellectual property company, focusing on acquiring film and television assets, running a regional movie theater, and licensing access to an NFT-based marketplace platform for movies and music. The company rebranded from AB International Group Corp in December 2025, signaling a shift toward contemporary media distribution channels. Its two business segments—copyrights and licensing (IP) and cinema operations—reflect a dual strategy of owning distributable content while maintaining physical exhibition presence. AERA trades on the [OTC Pink](/otc-pink/) Sheets with minimal liquidity, typical of early-stage or restructuring media enterprises with limited institutional participation.

## Assets, Control, and Liquidity Constraints

The company's value rests primarily on its acquired film and TV library and the licensing rights to an NFT marketplace platform, supplemented by the Mount Kisco theater and ABQQ.tv, a direct-to-consumer streaming service. Chiyuan Deng, as President and sole director, holds Series A [Preferred Stock](/preferred-stock/) granting 51% voting control, concentrating strategic decisions in one shareholder's hands. The company pays no [dividend](/wiki/dividend/) and operates without significant analyst coverage or institutional ownership—typical of OTC micro-caps where trading is sparse and [price discovery](/price-discovery/) is limited. Success depends on executing IP [acquisition](/acquisition/) strategy, maintaining the streaming platform, and effectively distributing content through both traditional and digital channels.
