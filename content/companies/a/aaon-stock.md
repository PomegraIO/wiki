*AAON, Inc. (ticker **AAON**) is a manufacturer of heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) equipment, rooftop units, and air handling systems for commercial and industrial buildings, as well as related controls and accessories.*

## What the company does

AAON manufactures a range of HVAC equipment including rooftop packaged units (self-contained air conditioning and heating systems installed on building roofs), air handlers, heating units, and related controls and components. The company's products serve commercial buildings, light industrial facilities, warehouses, and other non-residential structures.

Products are engineered for energy efficiency, reliability, and ease of installation and service. AAON sells primarily to HVAC contractors and mechanical integrators who design systems for building owners and install AAON equipment as part of larger mechanical systems.

## How it makes money

AAON generates revenue from the sale of HVAC equipment units, components, and controls. Pricing is influenced by equipment specifications (capacity, efficiency ratings, features), competitive supply dynamics, and input material costs (particularly steel, copper, and aluminum).

Gross profit depends on manufacturing efficiency, production scale, material cost management, and the product mix sold. The company manufactures equipment at facilities and sells primarily to HVAC contractors and distributors who then resell to end-users. Gross margins are affected by manufacturing yield, capacity utilization, and competitive pricing. Operating expenses include R&D for product efficiency improvement, sales and marketing, and administrative overhead.

## Where it sits in its industry

AAON competes in the commercial HVAC equipment market alongside larger multinational companies like Johnson Controls, Carrier (Trane Technologies), and regional manufacturers. The industry is characterized by established product performance standards, energy efficiency regulations, and consolidation around several large players.

Competitive advantages for AAON include manufacturing efficiency, product reliability and reputation, engineering expertise in efficiency improvements, and distribution relationships. Industry trends include increasing focus on energy efficiency (driven by building codes and sustainability goals), smart controls and connectivity, and life-cycle cost optimization by building owners and operators.
