**A golf and hospitality operator with deep roots in Florida's leisure landscape.** Aureus Greenway Holdings owns and operates public golf country clubs across Florida, built around 18-hole championship courses and complementary amenities. The company derives revenue from membership fees, green fees, food and beverage sales in its clubhouses, equipment rentals, pro shop operations, and ancillary services that span tournament hosting to golf instruction.

## Properties and Service Mix

The company's core assets are its country club properties, which function as integrated golf-and-lifestyle destinations. Each location features an 18-hole course, fairway ranges for practice, a clubhouse with restaurant and bar service, a pro shop stocked with golf equipment and apparel, and membership tiers that unlock discounts and privileges. Non-members can still access the courses through daily green fee play. Revenue flows from both high-margin membership dues and transactional fees (rounds played, club rentals, lessons, tournament entry fees, and hospitality spending).

## Capital Structure and Market Position

Aureus Greenway was incorporated in 2023 and went public on the [Nasdaq Capital Market](/wiki/stock-exchange/) in February 2025 under ticker AGH, becoming a smaller-cap public company in the leisure and hospitality sector. The company has pursued capital raises to fund operations and growth—including a July 2025 equity offering of approximately $26 million. As a discretionary-spending business in a cyclical consumer environment, membership-driven country clubs carry vulnerability to economic downturns and interest-rate-sensitive household spending patterns, though the membership model can provide revenue stability compared to daily-fee-only operators.
