*Accel Entertainment, Inc. (**ACEL**) is a gaming and entertainment company that owns and operates video gaming locations and provides entertainment services. The company operates in the gaming and entertainment sector, managing facilities with video gaming machines and related entertainment amenities in multiple regulated markets.*

## What the company does

Accel Entertainment owns and operates video gaming locations including bars, restaurants, and entertainment venues equipped with gaming machines. The company derives revenue from video gaming machine placement, commissions from gaming activity, and management of entertainment venues. Operations include managing gaming locations, maintaining gaming equipment, handling customer service, and ensuring regulatory compliance.

The company's venues combine entertainment activities including gaming machines with food and beverage service, creating destination locations for entertainment and social activities. Venue management encompasses staff hiring and training, equipment maintenance, customer experience management, and regulatory compliance with gaming authorities.

## Gaming and entertainment market

The gaming and entertainment sector encompasses diverse activities ranging from traditional casino operations to video gaming venues in bars, restaurants, and entertainment centers. Video gaming machines are regulated products subject to licensing and regulatory oversight in each jurisdiction where they operate.

Accel Entertainment's business model focuses on venue-based gaming rather than large casino properties, providing entertainment and social experiences in more accessible neighborhood venues. Market demand is influenced by consumer discretionary spending, entertainment preferences, and regulatory environment in each jurisdiction.

## Regulatory environment

Gaming operations are heavily regulated, with each state and locality maintaining specific regulations governing gaming machine placement, operation, customer protections, and revenue sharing. Accel must maintain appropriate licenses, comply with gaming regulations, implement responsible gaming programs, and remit required taxes and fees to gaming authorities.

Regulatory compliance requires training staff on responsible gaming, maintaining detailed records, preventing underage gaming access, and implementing controls ensuring game integrity and player protection. Regulatory changes or stricter enforcement could impact operations and profitability.

## Revenue and operating model

Accel generates revenue primarily from gaming machine operation, including player spending less regulatory fees and operator commissions. Additional revenue comes from food and beverage services at managed venues. Expenses include staff compensation, equipment maintenance, utilities, regulatory fees and taxes, and venue operating costs.

Profitability depends on gaming machine utilization rates, player spending patterns, venue operating efficiency, and the regulatory tax and fee structure in each jurisdiction. Location selection, customer experience management, and marketing drive traffic and player activity.

## Competitive landscape and market positioning

Video gaming venues compete with other entertainment and leisure activities including traditional casinos, online gaming, sports betting, and other discretionary entertainment options. Accel competes with other gaming venue operators based on location, venue atmosphere, gaming machine variety, service quality, and customer experience.

Market consolidation and regulatory changes influence competitive dynamics. The legalization of sports betting and expansion of other gaming options in various jurisdictions affects competitive positioning and market dynamics for traditional video gaming venues.
