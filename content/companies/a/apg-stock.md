**APi Group serves critical-safety infrastructure—fire suppression, electronic security, and elevators—across infrastructure, healthcare, and tech facilities.**

APi Group operates two main business segments. Its Safety Services division installs, inspects, services, and monitors fire protection systems, electronic security platforms, and escalators and elevators in data centers, hospitals, manufacturing plants, and distribution facilities. Specialty Services handles higher-margin contracting work: fabrication and infrastructure services tied to power utilities, telecommunications networks, and essential facilities that need custom engineering support.

The company was founded in 1926 and operates globally from its headquarters in New Brighton, Minnesota. It emerged into its current form through a [reverse merger](/reverse-merger/) in 2019 when J2 [Acquisition](/acquisition/) Limited acquired and rebranded as APi Group, then went public on the NYSE under ticker APG.

**Revenue and positioning.** APi Group sits in the fragmented safety and facilities services market. Its recurring-service model—inspection schedules, monitoring contracts, elevator maintenance plans—creates a base of predictable cash flow. The specialty contracting arm operates on project margins and capital intensity, competing on engineering capability and execution in hard-to-reach infrastructure. The company's customers include Fortune 500 firms managing mission-critical assets where downtime or safety failure carries existential risk.

**At a glance**

- Dual revenue streams: recurring safety-service contracts plus project-based specialty contracting
- Operates in regulated markets (fire codes, elevator inspection standards, security certification)
- End markets: critical infrastructure, data centers, hospitals, utilities, manufacturing
- Global footprint with regional service networks essential to the recurring-service model
- Recurring revenue provides margin stability; specialty work offers higher single-project returns
