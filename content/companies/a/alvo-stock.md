*[**Alvotech**](/wiki/public-company/) (**ALVO**) is an Icelandic [biopharmaceutical](/wiki/biopharmaceutical/) company specializing in the development and commercialization of [biosimilar](/wiki/biosimilar/) medications. The company focuses on creating cost-effective alternatives to expensive branded biologic drugs, targeting multiple therapeutic areas including oncology, gastroenterology, and rheumatology.*

## What the company does

Alvotech is a biopharmaceutical company dedicated to developing biosimilar medications. Biosimilars are highly similar versions of existing biologic drugs whose [patents](/wiki/patent/) have expired, created to provide therapeutic equivalence at lower costs. The company operates in a competitive space where it seeks regulatory approval from authorities including the U.S. [FDA](/wiki/fda/) and [EMA](/wiki/ema/) for its biosimilar candidates.

The company's pipeline includes biosimilar candidates targeting conditions such as cancer, inflammatory bowel disease, and autoimmune disorders. By targeting high-value biologic medications, Alvotech aims to capture significant market share as these branded drugs lose patent protection.

## How it makes money

Alvotech's revenue model depends on successfully bringing biosimilar products to market and achieving regulatory approval. The company generates revenue from product sales once biosimilars are approved and commercialized. Additionally, Alvotech may pursue partnerships, licensing agreements, or other collaborations with larger pharmaceutical companies to fund development and commercialization efforts.

The business model relies on the [clinical trial](/wiki/clinical-trial/) process to demonstrate bioequivalence and safety of its biosimilar candidates, followed by regulatory submissions and approvals. Earlier-stage revenues may come from out-licensing opportunities or development milestones paid by partners.

## Where it sits in its industry

Alvotech competes in the biosimilar market alongside larger established players and other specialized biosimilar developers. The biosimilar industry has grown as major biologic drugs have lost patent protection, creating opportunities for lower-cost alternatives. The company faces competition from multinational pharmaceutical firms with greater resources, as well as other dedicated biosimilar companies.

Regulatory approval and manufacturing expertise are critical competitive advantages in this space. Alvotech's focus on specific therapeutic areas and its ability to navigate complex regulatory pathways determine its competitive position. The biosimilar sector benefits from structural tailwinds—aging biologic patents and healthcare payers seeking cost containment—but remains subject to regulatory scrutiny and competitive intensity.
