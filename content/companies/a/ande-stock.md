Andersons, Inc. is a diversified agricultural and renewable fuels company operating a network of grain elevators, commodity trading operations, and ethanol production facilities across North America. Built on the foundation of grain merchandising and logistics, the company has expanded into agricultural inputs and renewable energy, positioning itself as a critical link in the agricultural supply chain.

The business draws revenues from two primary operational pillars. The agribusiness segment handles the physical movement and merchandising of agricultural commodities—corn, wheat, soybeans, and processed feed ingredients—alongside the manufacture and distribution of crop nutrients and fertilizers. The renewables segment focuses on ethanol and co-product production, capturing value from commodity price spreads and government support programs.

Geographically, Andersons operates across the United States, Canada, and Mexico, with particular concentration in the grain belt. The company maintains an asset-intensive footprint of elevators and processing facilities that serve as collection and logistics points for farmers and distribution hubs for customers.

| Business Area | Description |
|---|---|
| **Commodity Merchandising** | Grain trading, storage, and logistics for corn, wheat, soybeans |
| **Agricultural Inputs** | Fertilizer manufacturing, distribution, and wholesale nutrient sales |
| **Feed & Ingredients** | Processing and selling specialty feed products and grain ingredients |
| **Ethanol & Renewables** | Ethanol production and co-product sales (dried distillers grains) |

The company's profitability depends on the margin between commodity purchase prices and sales prices, the efficiency of its physical asset network, and favorable conditions in input markets and biofuel production. Capacity utilization, fertilizer demand cycles, and ethanol crack spreads all shape operating results. Andersons is exposed to agricultural commodity price volatility, farmer cash flow conditions, and the policy environment affecting renewable fuel mandates.
