ADM Tronics Unlimited, Inc. is a technology-based developer and manufacturer spanning three distinct business areas: proprietary medical devices, electronics engineering and manufacturing services, and environmentally focused chemical formulations. Incorporated in 1969 and headquartered in Northvale, New Jersey, the company operates with a portfolio that reflects its evolution from electronics vendor to a diversified technology shop spanning diagnostics, industrial electronics, and specialty chemistry.

The company's electronics division centers on non-invasive medical devices for diagnostic and therapeutic applications in human and animal healthcare, plus electronic controllers for the spa and hot tub industry. This segment serves markets across the United States, Australia, Asia, and Europe. The medical devices focus on proprietary technology for applications where non-invasive measurement or treatment offers value.

Its engineering and manufacturing services segment provides design, prototyping, regulatory consultation, and contract manufacturing for clients needing specialized electronics expertise. This capability-for-hire model allows the company to monetize its accumulated technical staff and facilities between product development cycles.

The chemicals division produces water-based formulations marketed as environmentally preferable alternatives to solvent-based products. These include primers, adhesives, coatings, resins, conductive paints, and anti-static products. The portfolio extends to specialized topical and adhesive formulations for maxillofacial prosthetic applications and to professional makeup used in film, television, and theatrical production.

As a diversified microcap operator with roots in the electronics industry, ADM Tronics maintains a presence on the [OTC Markets](/wiki/stock/) under the ticker ADMT, trading in the pink sheets. The company's structure reflects decades of product-line accumulation and strategic pivots toward both medical device niche markets and industrial coatings where water-based chemistry meets regulatory and environmental demand.
