*[Afya Ltd](/wiki/public-company/) (**AFYA**) is a [US-listed](/wiki/stock/) Brazilian healthcare education company serving the medical education sector. Operating primarily in Brazil, Afya provides undergraduate medical programs, postgraduate and residency training, continuing education, and specialized clinical services to medical professionals and institutions.*

## What the company does

Afya operates a network of medical schools and educational institutions across Brazil. The company offers comprehensive medical education programs ranging from undergraduate degrees to postgraduate residency training and continuing professional development. Beyond traditional classroom education, Afya provides digital learning platforms, clinical services through teaching hospitals and affiliated clinics, and specialized training in various medical disciplines. The company serves students, physicians, and healthcare institutions. Afya's integrated model combines education delivery with clinical operations, creating feedback loops where clinical experience enhances teaching quality. This vertical integration differentiates Afya from standalone educational providers and creates switching costs for students and physicians.

## How it makes money

Afya generates revenue from tuition fees collected from medical students and residents attending its undergraduate and graduate programs. The company also earns revenue from continuing education and professional development courses, which serve practicing physicians. Clinical services—including laboratory work, diagnostic procedures, and consultations—provided through affiliated medical centers generate additional income. The diversified revenue model reduces dependence on any single educational product or customer segment.

## Market position and growth drivers

Brazil has a growing population and expanding healthcare demand, creating opportunities for medical education providers. Afya benefits from the country's physician shortage and increasing demand for professional development. The company's size and brand recognition position it as a major consolidator in Brazilian medical education. Regulatory approval and accreditation standards are important—medical education is heavily regulated, and maintaining accreditation across programs is essential to Afya's operations. Population growth and rising middle-class healthcare consumption create underlying tailwinds for physician demand. Afya's geographic footprint across Brazil allows it to serve patients and students in multiple regions, reducing dependence on any single market.

## Industry and competitive dynamics

The medical education sector in Brazil is fragmented but increasingly consolidating. Afya competes with other private medical schools and state-run institutions. The company's competitive advantages include scale, diverse program offerings, integrated clinical operations, and brand reputation. Digital transformation in education—accelerated by global trends—is reshaping how medical training is delivered, creating both opportunities and challenges for traditional classroom-based providers.
