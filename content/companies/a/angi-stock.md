*Angi Inc.* (**ANGI**) is a US-listed home services marketplace that operates digital platforms connecting homeowners with contractors and service professionals. The company operates in the broader consumer services and home improvement sector, facilitating transactions across renovation, repair, and ongoing maintenance categories.

## What the company does

Angi operates digital platforms that serve as intermediaries in the home services market. The company connects homeowners seeking repairs, remodeling, or routine maintenance with a vetted network of contractors, electricians, plumbers, and other service providers. Its platforms allow consumers to post service requests, receive quotes, and manage interactions with professionals, while contractors use the platform to find customers and manage their businesses.

## How it makes money

Angi generates revenue primarily through a service fee or commission model. Contractors and service professionals pay subscription fees or commission-based payments to access customer leads generated through the platform. The company monetizes both the supply side (contractors paying to reach customers) and consumer-facing services. It may also generate ancillary revenue through advertising and premium service tiers that offer enhanced visibility or priority access to leads.

## Scale and market position

The home services market is large and fragmented, characterized by many small, independent contractors operating in local markets. Angi's platforms aim to aggregate supply and demand across geographies, creating network effects that benefit both sides. The company competes with other digital marketplaces in the home services space, as well as with traditional offline and direct-to-consumer service providers. Its value proposition depends on maintaining both contractor quality and consumer acquisition and retention.

## Business model dynamics

Angi faces typical two-sided marketplace economics. Growth depends on building critical mass on both the consumer and contractor sides. Consumer acquisition costs are a key operational metric, as are contractor retention rates and the quality of matches made on the platform. The business is sensitive to housing market activity, consumer discretionary spending, and the willingness of service professionals to adopt digital channels for customer acquisition.
