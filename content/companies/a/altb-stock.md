*[Alpine Auto Brokers Inc.](/wiki/public-company/) (**ALTB**) is a [wholesale](/wiki/wholesale-definition/) automotive broker that facilitates the purchase and sale of used vehicles between dealers, auctions, and auto remarketing operations. The company operates in the automotive services sector, providing a marketplace platform where dealers and other market participants can buy and sell vehicles.*

## What the company does

Alpine Auto Brokers operates as an intermediary in the used vehicle market. The company's core business involves buying and selling vehicles from dealerships, auctions, fleet operations, and other sources. Rather than holding inventory long-term, it acts as a broker—connecting sellers with buyers and facilitating transactions in the wholesale automotive channel. This marketplace model allows dealers and remarketing operations to liquify used vehicle inventory and access vehicles for resale.

## How it makes money

The company generates revenue primarily through transaction fees and commissions on vehicles sold through its platform. Fees may be charged to sellers, buyers, or both parties depending on transaction type and customer arrangements. Revenue can also come from value-added services such as vehicle inspection, reconditioning coordination, title handling, and logistics support. The wholesale automotive market is driven by dealer turnover rates, fleet disposition cycles, and overall vehicle supply dynamics.

## Where it sits in its industry

Alpine Auto Brokers operates within a competitive landscape of automotive auctions, online marketplaces, and wholesale dealers. Unlike traditional [auction](/wiki/auction-market/) houses that hold physical events, the company may operate with a hybrid or digital-first model depending on its operational structure. The wholesale automotive market has consolidated around several large players including regional and national auction operators. Alpine's positioning depends on its geographic reach, customer relationships, technology platform, and operational efficiency relative to larger competitors.
