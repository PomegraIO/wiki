Alpine Auto Brokers connects buyers and sellers in the wholesale automotive market, functioning as an intermediary platform in the used vehicle channel. Rather than operating traditional retail dealerships, the company facilitates transactions between dealerships, auction houses, fleet operators, and other commercial market participants seeking to buy and sell pre-owned vehicles efficiently.

The company's revenue model centers on transaction fees and commissions earned on vehicles moving through its marketplace or brokerage services. By positioning itself as a dealer-to-dealer connector, Alpine captures value from transaction efficiency, reduced holding periods for inventory, and the coordination of vehicle flow between supply sources and end retailers. Additional revenue streams may come from ancillary services including vehicle inspection, documentation, title processing, and logistics facilitation.

Alpine's operations depend on vehicle supply dynamics, dealer inventory turnover rates, and fleet disposition cycles. As a wholesale market participant, the company faces cyclical demand tied to new vehicle sales, economic conditions affecting dealer profitability, and competition from traditional auction houses and emerging digital vehicle marketplace platforms.
