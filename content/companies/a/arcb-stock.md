*[**ARCBEST CORP /DE/** (**ARCB**)](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0000894405) is a transportation and logistics holding company offering freight services, logistics solutions, and specialized trucking operations across North America. The company operates through multiple segments serving regional and national customers in diverse industries.*

## What the company does

ARCBEST operates as a diversified transportation and logistics provider. The company manages freight transportation operations, including regional and long-haul trucking, alongside logistics and supply chain solutions that leverage both company-owned equipment and third-party carrier networks. Its business model includes asset-based trucking operations and asset-light logistics services, allowing it to serve customers with varying transportation needs from full-truckload moves to less-than-truckload services and specialized logistics support.

## How it makes money

The company generates revenue across transportation and logistics services. Asset-based operations generate fees from freight movements using owned tractors and trailers. Asset-light logistics operations capitalize on brokerage and third-party networks, with margin compression and expansion depending on carrier availability and rate environments. Different segments serve different market niches—specialized services command pricing power in niche markets, while commodity transportation is volume-dependent and more price-sensitive. Customers span industrial manufacturers, retail, consumer goods, and logistics intermediaries.

## Where it sits in its industry

ARCBEST is a mid-cap player in North American freight and logistics, competing with larger integrated carriers, smaller regional players, and non-asset-based logistics brokers. Unlike full-service integrated carriers, it maintains both asset and non-asset segments, giving flexibility but requiring operational complexity. The transportation sector is cyclical, sensitive to economic growth, consumer spending, and industrial production. Operating leverage in asset-based businesses means that revenue swings translate into larger profit swings, particularly in downturns.
