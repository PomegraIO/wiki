*[**Authentic Holdings, Inc.**](/wiki/public-company/) (ticker **AHRO**) is a diversified holding company engaged in the acquisition, development, and monetization of entertainment content, collectibles, and digital assets. The company operates across multiple business segments including streaming media, entertainment marketing, music recordings, digital collectibles, and experiential events.*

## What the company does

Authentic Holdings operates as a diversified holding company with interests spanning entertainment, music, collectibles, and digital media. The company's portfolio includes subsidiaries focused on streaming entertainment, apparel technology, music licensing, digital collectibles (NFTs), and live events. The company describes itself as pursuing "vertical synergies" across its operating units to generate revenue through content licensing, merchandise, and digital platforms.

The company's operations reach audiences through multiple channels: streaming partnerships with platforms reaching hundreds of millions of devices, limited-edition merchandise incorporating memorabilia fibers, licensed music master recordings, and Web3-based digital collectibles tied to entertainment properties.

## How it makes money

Revenue generation flows through several business units. The entertainment subsidiary operates streaming content platforms. The collectibles division produces limited-edition apparel items. The music and recordings unit licenses master recordings from its owned catalog to digital platforms and media companies. The digital collectibles segment develops membership-based NFT products. Events operations generate revenue through experiential entertainment offerings. The company has emphasized compound revenue growth across these segments and IP monetization as core drivers.

## Where it sits in its industry

Authentic Holdings operates at the intersection of traditional entertainment, collectibles, and emerging digital media. The company competes with streaming services, music licensing firms, collectibles merchants, and Web3 content platforms. Its holding company structure allows it to operate independently across sectors—music licensing companies, entertainment marketing firms, digital media startups, and collectibles producers—rather than competing as a single integrated entertainment studio.

The company's strategy centers on acquiring and bundling entertainment intellectual property, master recordings, and branded content that can be monetized through multiple channels. This differs from traditional media companies focused on single-format distribution or specialty retailers limited to physical goods.
