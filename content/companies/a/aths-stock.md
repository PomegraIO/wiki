*[Athene Holding Ltd.](/wiki/aths-stock/) (**ATHS**) is a global insurance and financial services holding company headquartered in Bermuda and dual-listed on the New York Stock Exchange. The firm specializes in retirement savings solutions, structured annuities, and alternative investment management, serving institutional investors, insurance companies, and retail customers across North America and international markets.*

## What the company does

Athene operates as a diversified financial services and insurance holding company with three core business lines. The company writes structured annuities and retirement income solutions for institutional investors, manages alternative investments, and maintains a portfolio of liabilities acquired from other insurers—primarily fixed annuities and structured settlements. Its operations span the United States, Europe, and parts of Asia-Pacific. Athene's business model centers on sourcing capital-efficient insurance liabilities while managing them through investment strategies designed to match duration and generate spreads.

## How it makes money

Athene generates revenue primarily from net investment income, including the spread between yields on its investment portfolio and the crediting rates it pays to annuity holders. Premium collections from its annuity products contribute to net income, and fees from alternative investment management add to revenue streams. The company also benefits from the longevity and persistency of its annuity book—longer lives and lower lapse rates improve profitability. Interest spread compression, rising mortality, or increased surrender rates can reduce margins. Acquisition of insurance blocks from competitors is a core capital deployment strategy, and successful integration of those blocks supports long-term earnings growth.

## Where it sits in its industry

Athene competes in the global insurance and [retirement savings](/wiki/401k-plan/) market alongside larger diversified insurers, monoline annuity writers, and private asset managers. The company maintains a smaller footprint than giants such as Prudential or MetLife, but positions itself as a specialist in managing closed books of business and structured annuities—a lower-growth but capital-efficient niche. Its alternative investment platform and focus on liability-driven investing appeal to institutional investors. Scale in alternative assets, operational efficiency in annuity administration, and relationships with institutional sellers of insurance blocks are competitive advantages. Rising interest rates, which reduce the value of legacy fixed-income businesses, and competitive pressure on annuity pricing pose industry-wide headwinds.

## Capital structure and financial position

Athene maintains a holding company structure with regulated insurance subsidiaries and capital management policies designed to preserve flexibility for acquisitions. Debt levels reflect the company's capital-intensive insurance model, and surplus capital is typically deployed into new block acquisitions. The firm monitors regulatory capital ratios and solvency requirements across its operating jurisdictions. Investors should examine the composition of the investment portfolio (duration, credit quality, illiquid alternatives) and estimates of reserve adequacy for legacy business lines.

Industry reports from rating agencies and insurance-focused research firms provide benchmarks for capital ratios and returns on equity compared to peers. Announcements of insurance block acquisitions—whether Athene is buyer or seller—signal strategic direction and capital deployment priorities.
