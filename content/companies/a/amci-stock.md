**AMC Robotics designs and manufactures automated systems for material handling, warehouse operations, and industrial production lines.** The company provides robotic equipment and integration services to fulfill complex logistics needs and manufacturing processes where precision, consistency, and speed drive competitive advantage.

## The hardware business

AMC Robotics sells robotic systems ranging from collaborative arms for small-scale assembly to large integrated systems for high-throughput warehousing. Customers include logistics operators managing e-commerce fulfillment, automotive factories requiring precision assembly, and electronics manufacturers needing flexible automation. Revenue comes primarily from equipment sales, with customization and integration adding value per deployment.

## Service and maintenance

Recurring revenue comes from service contracts, software updates, spare parts, and technical support. Once installed, these systems require ongoing optimization and repair—a stable income stream that helps offset the lumpiness of equipment sales. Many customers structure their capital equipment purchases with bundled service agreements that run multiple years.

## At a glance

- Manufactures collaborative and industrial robotic systems
- Serves automotive, electronics, packaging, and logistics sectors
- Revenue mix: equipment sales (~70%), integration services, maintenance contracts (~30%)
- Faces competition from ABB, KUKA, Fanuc, and emerging collaborative robotics firms
- Growth tied to manufacturing investment cycles and e-commerce logistics demand
