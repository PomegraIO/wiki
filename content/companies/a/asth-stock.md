*Astrana Health, Inc. (ASTH) is a health maintenance organization that delivers integrated healthcare services and insurance coverage. Operating as a direct primary care provider and managed care organization, the company serves patients across multiple states, combining insurance administration with direct clinical delivery.*

## What the company does

Astrana Health operates as an integrated healthcare delivery and financing platform. The company combines traditional health insurance offerings with owned and affiliated primary care clinics, creating a vertically integrated model that bridges the gap between patients and healthcare providers. This integration allows the organization to manage both the financial and clinical aspects of healthcare delivery.

The company serves multiple populations including individual and family plan members, employer groups, and government-sponsored programs. Its service territory spans a geographically diverse footprint, with particular depth in certain regional markets. By directly employing or partnering with primary care physicians and medical professionals, Astrana creates touchpoints throughout the patient care continuum.

## How it makes money

Astrana's revenue model operates on two primary streams. The first comes from premium revenue—insurance premiums collected from members and employer groups for healthcare coverage. The company bears the risk of member healthcare costs and generates profit when medical spending falls below collected premiums. The second stream derives from the owned and affiliated clinic operations, which generate visit fees and ancillary service revenue directly.

The direct primary care component creates additional incentives for cost management. By controlling clinic locations and employing physicians, the company aligns financial performance with clinical outcomes. When the same organization bears both insurance risk and operates treatment facilities, there is inherent pressure to deliver efficient, preventive-focused care that reduces downstream medical spending.

## Market position and competitive dynamics

The HMO industry is characterized by substantial consolidation and regional variation. Larger national managed care organizations operate across numerous states with diverse membership bases. Astrana competes in this landscape as a regional to mid-sized player, with strategic positioning around integrated care delivery. The company's model—combining insurance underwriting with owned clinical assets—represents one approach to vertical integration within healthcare.

Insurance margin pressure, medical cost inflation, and regulatory oversight shape competitive dynamics across the sector. Health maintenance organizations face ongoing pressure to balance premium growth with claims management, maintain service quality, and comply with state insurance regulation. Provider consolidation, network adequacy requirements, and evolving payment models continue to reshape competitive positioning in the market.
