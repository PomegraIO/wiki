*[AKAMAI TECHNOLOGIES INC](/wiki/public-company/) (**AKAM**) is a content delivery and edge computing platform that manages traffic routing, accelerates digital service performance, and provides security solutions. The company operates one of the world's largest globally distributed computing platforms, serving enterprises, media companies, and developers from thousands of locations worldwide.*

## What the company does

Akamai operates a global network platform that handles traffic routing, content acceleration, and security operations for digital services. The platform interconnects with thousands of networks and devices worldwide, allowing it to cache and distribute content closer to end users, reduce latency, and mitigate distributed denial-of-service attacks. The company serves customers across media, entertainment, e-commerce, financial services, telecommunications, and enterprise markets.

The technical foundation involves edge computing—executing logic and storing data at the network periphery rather than centralized data centers. Akamai's footprint includes servers, points of presence, and interconnection points distributed across geographies to handle traffic spikes, improve response times, and enhance security posture.

## How it makes money

The company generates revenue primarily through platform subscription and usage-based services. Core business lines include content delivery (which accelerates streaming, downloads, and web experiences), security services (DDoS protection, web application firewalls, bot management), and traffic control solutions. Enterprise customers typically commit to annual contracts with performance-based pricing that scales with data volume and feature complexity.

Media and entertainment customers use the platform to deliver video streaming at scale. E-commerce and financial services rely on it for transaction acceleration and security. Smaller organizations access services through self-service portals with consumption-based pricing. The company operates at high gross margins given the software-driven nature of its core platform, with operating costs concentrated in network infrastructure maintenance, personnel, and development.

## Where it sits in its industry

Akamai competes in edge computing and [cloud services](/wiki/cloud-services/) alongside providers like Cloudflare, AWS CloudFront, and Fastly. The broader industry encompasses content delivery networks, application performance management, and cybersecurity—all increasingly converging as companies move workloads toward edge architecture.

The company's main competitive advantages rest on scale (the size and geographic distribution of its network), decades of operational expertise in managing global traffic, and bundled service offerings that lock in customer dependencies. Competitors emphasize different strengths: some offer lower pricing through simpler feature sets, others embed services within larger cloud ecosystems.
