*[AlTi Global, Inc.](/wiki/public-company/) (**ALTI**) is a specialty chemicals and advanced materials manufacturer headquartered in the United States. The company serves aerospace, industrial, and transportation sectors with a focus on high-performance thermal management, composite systems, and other engineering materials.*

## What the company does

AlTi Global manufactures and supplies specialty chemical products and advanced materials serving demanding industrial markets. The company's product portfolio centers on thermal management systems, aerospace-grade composites, and engineered solutions for extreme operating environments. Its offerings include specialty resins, adhesives, coatings, and fiber-reinforced composite materials designed for durability, heat resistance, and structural performance.

The company operates through production facilities and research capabilities designed to meet stringent aerospace, defense, and industrial specifications. Its technical focus addresses mission-critical applications where material performance failures create safety or operational risk.

## How it makes money

AlTi Global generates revenue through the sale of specialty chemical formulations and composite systems to aerospace manufacturers, industrial equipment producers, and component suppliers. The company markets through direct relationships with major OEMs and tier-one suppliers in aerospace and defense, as well as industrial manufacturers requiring high-performance thermal and structural solutions.

Gross margins benefit from technical differentiation and customer switching costs inherent in aerospace qualification and defense supplier approval. Revenue concentration typically favors long-term contracts with established customers, creating visibility into forward demand.

## Strategic positioning

The company operates in the specialty chemicals sector, where scale, technical expertise, and customer relationships create competitive advantages. Aerospace and defense represent high-margin markets with significant barriers to entry due to regulatory qualification, quality certifications, and long development cycles for new materials.

AlTi competes against both larger diversified chemical companies and smaller specialized competitors. Its positioning depends on maintaining technical leadership in thermal management and composite technology, holding qualified supplier status with major aerospace customers, and managing supply chain reliability and production capacity to meet customer demand windows.

## Industry context

The aerospace and defense markets drive demand for advanced materials and thermal management solutions. Commercial aviation, military aircraft, space systems, and defense platforms all rely on high-performance materials to meet fuel efficiency, weight reduction, safety, and operational temperature targets. These markets reward innovation in materials science and long-term customer relationships.

Industrial thermal management also serves power generation, automotive, and other sectors requiring reliable high-temperature performance. Competition in specialty chemicals operates around differentiation, not commodity pricing, with customers evaluating materials science expertise, supply reliability, and total cost of ownership across extended product lifecycles.
