*[Atlantic Union Bankshares Corp](/wiki/public-company/) **(AUB)** is a [US-listed](/wiki/stock-exchange/) [bank holding company](/wiki/bank-holding-company/) that provides [retail](/wiki/retail-banking/) and [commercial banking](/wiki/commercial-banking/) services across the Mid-Atlantic United States.*

## What the company does

Atlantic Union operates subsidiary banks that serve customers in Virginia, Maryland, Delaware, Pennsylvania, and New Jersey. The organization functions as a [holding company](/wiki/holding-company/) for multiple independent bank franchises, each operating under its own brand. The subsidiary banks offer deposit products, loan services, and wealth management to individual consumers, small and medium-sized businesses, and institutional clients. The company maintains a network of banking centers and provides services through digital and mobile platforms.

## How it makes money

The company generates [net interest income](/wiki/net-interest-income/) through the spread between what it pays on deposits and other borrowings versus what it earns on loans and investment securities. [Noninterest income](/wiki/noninterest-income/) comes from [loan origination fees](/wiki/loan-origination-fee/), [deposit service charges](/wiki/deposit-service-charges/), [wealth management](/wiki/wealth-management-fee/) fees, and investment advisory services. Like other regional banks, the company's profitability depends on the interest rate environment, the health of its loan portfolio, and its ability to manage operating expenses.

## Business model and operations

Atlantic Union structures itself as a multi-brand [bank holding company](/wiki/bank-holding-company/) rather than operating as a single consolidated brand. This allows each subsidiary bank to maintain local autonomy, preserve customer relationships under established names, and compete effectively in their respective regional markets. The subsidiary banks underwrite loans, originate mortgages, manage trust and investment services, and gather deposits. The holding company provides capital, regulatory compliance oversight, and risk management functions that are typically consolidated at the parent level.

## Competition and market position

The Mid-Atlantic banking market is competitive, with regional and national banks competing for deposits and loan customers. Atlantic Union competes on relationship banking, local market knowledge, and personalized service. The region includes presence of larger [megabanks](/wiki/megabank/) as well as other regional and community banks. Regulatory capital requirements and [deposit insurance](/wiki/fdic-deposit-insurance/) limits mean that growth strategies typically involve [acquisition](/wiki/acquisition/) activity or organic expansion through new banking centers.
