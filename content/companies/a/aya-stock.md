*[Aya Gold & Silver Inc.](/wiki/stock/) **(AYA)** is a Canadian precious metals mining company focused on gold and silver extraction and development, with operations and projects in Morocco. The company operates in the [mining](/wiki/public-company/) and resource extraction sector, developing primary projects in the Anti-Atlas region.*

## What the company does

Aya Gold & Silver owns and operates precious metals mining assets, with a geographic focus on Morocco. The company's flagship asset is the Zgounder silver-gold mine, located east of Agadir in the Proterozoic Siroua Massif of the Anti-Atlas Range. This property covers approximately 378 square kilometers. Aya also maintains an 85% interest in the Boumadine polymetallic project, which remains in exploration and evaluation phases. The company's core business is extracting gold and silver ore from these properties and selling the refined metals into commodity markets.

## How it makes money

Aya generates revenue by mining precious metals—primarily silver and gold—and selling them at prevailing commodity prices. The Zgounder operation is the company's principal revenue-generating asset. Metal production is processed either at the mine site or through third-party facilities, then marketed to buyers in the global [precious metals market](/wiki/stock-exchange/). The company's profitability depends on production volumes, operating costs, and commodity prices for gold and silver, which [fluctuate in commodity markets](/wiki/commodity-futures/).

## Where it sits in its industry

Precious metals mining is a mature industry serving jewelry, industrial applications, and [investment demand](/wiki/asset-allocation/). Larger multinational miners like Newmont and Barrick Gold operate at significantly larger scale, but mid-tier producers like Aya focus on specific projects or regions to achieve competitive unit costs. Aya's position in Morocco provides access to geologically prospective ground in North Africa, a region with established mining infrastructure. The company competes for capital, skilled labor, and market access with other junior and mid-tier gold and silver producers worldwide.
