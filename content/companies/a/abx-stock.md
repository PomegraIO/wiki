Abacus Global Management, Inc. (ticker ABX) is a merchant banking and asset management company focused on making equity investments and managing private investment funds, particularly with exposure to emerging markets and developing economies. The firm structures and operates merchant banking transactions, deploying capital from its own account as well as from third-party limited partners in various fund vehicles. This hybrid model—combining principal investment alongside fund management activities—distinguishes Abacus from pure asset managers and creates exposure both to [carried interest](/carried-interest-compensation/) and to the underlying portfolio performance.

The company's business model centers on sourcing, underwriting, and managing equity investments typically in small to mid-market enterprises and special situation transactions across its target geographies. Rather than charging [management fees](/management-fee/) alone, Abacus generates returns through a mix of management fees from funds it administers and carried interest on successful exits, supplemented by principal gains on its own capital deployed into deals. This creates alignment with limited partners but also concentrates risk: the firm's financial performance is tied directly to the success of its portfolio companies and its ability to identify and exit investments at favorable valuations.

Abacus operates in a competitive and capital-intensive sector where access to deal flow, operational expertise in emerging markets, and relationships with institutional investors drive competitive positioning. The firm must maintain sufficient [balance sheet](/balance-sheet/) capital to support both new deal origination and portfolio company support during downturns, while also managing redemptions and [capital calls](/capital-call/) from its fund investors. Fund performance is critical to its long-term viability; poor vintage-year performance can dry up fund-raising capacity and harm the fee-generating base.

The company faces several structural considerations common to merchant banking platforms: geographical [concentration risk](/concentration-risk/) in emerging markets, cyclicality of private equity distributions, the challenge of exiting large positions, and the dependency on key personnel retention in its deal origination and management functions. Like other merchant bankers, Abacus also operates with balance sheet leverage to enhance returns, which amplifies both gains and losses in economic downturns. Its capital structure and fund performance metrics appear in its [10-K](/wiki/10-k/) filings with the SEC.
