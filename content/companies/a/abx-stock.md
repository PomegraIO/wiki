## What the company does

Abacus Global Management operates as an investment adviser and asset manager, offering portfolio management services across multiple strategies and asset classes. The company may manage mutual funds, separate accounts for institutional investors, or hedge fund vehicles pursuing various investment objectives including long-only strategies, market-neutral approaches, or event-driven opportunities. Services typically include investment advice, portfolio construction, and ongoing monitoring.

## How it makes money

Investment management companies generate revenue through [management fees](/wiki/management-fee/) charged as a percentage of assets under management (AUM), typically ranging from 0.5% to 2% depending on strategy and client type. Performance fees, charged as a percentage of gains above a benchmark or hurdle rate, may supplement management fees. Revenue scales with assets under management and the fees charged to different client segments.

## Competitive landscape and positioning

Abacus competes with large diversified asset managers, specialized alternative investment firms, hedge fund managers, and financial advisory boutiques. Competition centers on investment performance, client relationships, brand reputation, and fee structures. Regulatory requirements and compliance obligations affect all market participants. The industry has seen consolidation driven by economies of scale and increasing regulatory costs.

## Assets under management and business model

The company's revenue and growth depend on its ability to attract and retain assets, generate investment performance that justifies fees charged, and control operating expenses. Market fluctuations affect AUM through changes in asset values and client redemptions. The company's success depends on generating returns competitive with alternatives available to investors and maintaining client relationships.
