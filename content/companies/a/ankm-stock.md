*Ankam, Inc.* (ANKM) is a Nevada-incorporated technology company that develops mobile applications and invests in financial and technology ventures. The company operates across North America and Asia, focusing on consumer-facing mobile platforms and strategic corporate investments.

## What the company does

Ankam, Inc. develops and operates mobile applications aimed at consumer finance and savings. Its primary product, Expense Minder, is a mobile application designed to streamline and manage expense reporting by allowing users to categorize transactions, set saving goals, receive bill reminders, and customize spending categories. The company also operates MoneySaverApp, a utility application that aggregates various discount cards on a mobile device, enabling users to access discounts from any location in real time. These applications are available through major app distribution platforms including the Apple App Store.

## How it makes money

The company's revenue model centers on consumer adoption of its mobile applications. Ankam likely derives revenue through subscription models, in-app purchases, or advertising integration within its applications. The Expense Minder and MoneySaverApp platforms generate income as users download and engage with the applications' features. The company is also engaged in strategic corporate investments and acquisitions that may generate returns through subsidiary operations and business development initiatives.

## Strategic expansion and subsidiaries

Ankam operates through several wholly-owned subsidiaries to manage its business lines and geographic reach. In November 2023, the company established Ankam LLC, a Wyoming-based subsidiary, to oversee operations for the MoneySaverApp. In January 2024, Ankam acquired complete ownership of Apex Intelligence LLC, which operates a currency converter service. The company expanded further in August 2024 by incorporating Mei Sheng Corporation Limited to establish a presence in Asian markets, particularly in Hong Kong and Taiwan. These subsidiaries reflect Ankam's strategy of building scalable operations across multiple geographic regions and diversifying its product offerings.

## Where it sits in its industry

Ankam competes in the crowded mobile application space, targeting personal finance management and consumer utilities. Expense management and financial tracking applications represent a mature segment with established competitors. The company differentiates through integration of multiple financial services into a unified platform. As an OTC-listed entity, Ankam operates with less regulatory oversight and market visibility compared to major exchange-listed software companies, though it remains subject to SEC reporting requirements through its public company status.
