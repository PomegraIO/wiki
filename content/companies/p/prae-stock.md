PRAE targets investors seeking growth exposure outside the traditional equity and [bond](/bond/) framework. The fund constructs a portfolio intended to deliver capital appreciation through a mix of alternative strategies—often including long equity exposure, credit positions, and other assets selected according to PlanRock's proprietary methodology. The approach aims to offer [diversification](/diversification/) from conventional stock indices while still capturing upside in growth.

Holdings typically span multiple asset classes and strategies. The fund may hold individual securities across regions and sectors, but the selection and weighting process is guided by PlanRock's alternative investment philosophy rather than by tracking a standard index. The goal is to identify opportunities where alternative techniques or less-conventional positioning might yield better returns than simple passive market exposure.

The exact composition shifts with market conditions and PlanRock's assessment of opportunity. Because the fund applies a systematic approach rather than pure discretion, the selection follows defined criteria updated as new market information becomes available. This can lead to concentrations in particular sectors, regions, or strategy types as the model identifies favourable conditions.

## Returns and risk profile

Alternative growth strategies typically carry higher volatility than balanced portfolios, though lower than pure equity exposure if diversification is working. PRAE's performance in any given year depends on which alternative bets are working—[credit spreads](/credit-spread/) widening, equity momentum, specific [sector rotations](/sector-rotation/), or other factors the underlying strategy captures. Years where traditional equities dominate returns may see PRAE lag if its alternative positioning is lighter. Conversely, periods where stocks struggle but credit and other assets perform may see PRAE hold up better.

Trading costs and strategy friction matter. Rebalancing a multi-asset portfolio regularly incurs transaction costs. If the underlying strategies involve less-liquid assets, the fund may face challenges in meeting redemptions without selling at disadvantageous prices.

[Model risk](/model-risk/) is embedded. The selection process depends on market relationships—valuation, volatility, sector rotations, [credit cycles](/credit-cycle/)—that can break or reverse. Backtested returns for alternative strategies are often higher than live performance once real capital is deployed, both because of model overfitting to past data and because the strategy's own trading affects market prices.

## Who invests in PRAE and why

PRAE suits investors who believe that stepping outside traditional equity and bond indices can improve risk-adjusted returns, and who trust PlanRock's ability to identify and execute alternative strategies. It appeals to those wanting growth without concentration in traditional large-cap stocks, and to portfolios seeking diversification from conventional passive indexing.

Investors researching PRAE should review the fund's prospectus and fact sheet, which explain the strategy, the types of alternative exposures, and the management philosophy. Examining the fund's actual holdings and their composition—what percentage is traditional equity, what percentage is credit or other alternatives—provides concrete detail beyond the marketing description. Performance data comparing PRAE to traditional equity indices and other alternative funds illuminates how the strategy has actually performed. Reading PlanRock's published materials on its investment approach and alternative methodology gives deeper insight into the firm's philosophy and execution.
