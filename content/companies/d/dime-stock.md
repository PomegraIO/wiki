CoinShares Altcoins ETF lets investors hold a basket of cryptocurrencies outside the [Bitcoin](/bitcoin/) and [Ethereum](/ethereum/) duopoly. The fund tracks an index of smaller blockchain tokens — projects built on [distributed ledgers](/distributed-ledger/), smart-contract platforms, and layer-2 networks — and trades on a major exchange with liquidity and transparency that holding coins directly does not offer. It is the fund-wrapper version of a bet on the diversity of the cryptocurrency ecosystem, for investors who want exposure to altcoins without managing private keys or picking individual projects.

## What the fund tracks

DIME targets an index of altcoins — typically tokens from established blockchain platforms and decentralized applications that are not Bitcoin or Ethereum. The exact composition changes, but often includes coins that power smart-contract chains, decentralized finance protocols, and layer-2 scaling solutions. Weights usually flow to [market cap](/market-capitalization/), so larger projects like Solana, Cardano, Polkadot, and Chainlink tend to be sizable holdings, while smaller positions rotate in and out as the cryptocurrency landscape evolves.

The fund does not hold Bitcoin or Ethereum directly. It is a pure-play altcoin vehicle. This makes it distinct from broad cryptocurrency exposure and filters the market for investors who believe innovation happens across a wider ecosystem.

## The structure and sponsor

CoinShares, a cryptocurrency asset-management firm headquartered in the UK, issues the fund as a standard ETF. DIME is not leveraged, inverse, or note-based. It trades on a major [stock exchange](/stock-exchange/) with standard [bid-ask spreads](/bid-ask-spread/) and intraday pricing. The fund holds cryptocurrency directly in custody arrangements operated by third parties, so investors do not manage wallets or exchange accounts themselves.

The fund rebalances periodically as the index methodology dictates — usually quarterly or as major composition shifts occur. Rebalancing introduces timing and liquidity friction into the process.

## Costs and who it is for

The [expense ratio](/expense-ratio/) is publicly stated and typically modest by active-management standards, though somewhat higher than a pure equity [index fund](/index-fund/) because cryptocurrency custody and index administration carry real overhead. Trading costs are separate: the coins themselves are volatile and illiquid, so the fund's bid-ask spread and daily volume reflect that volatility. Investors should expect wider spreads during stress periods.

DIME suits investors who believe the cryptocurrency ecosystem extends beyond Bitcoin and Ethereum and want that exposure without picking individual tokens. It appeals to pension funds, family offices, and retail investors who prefer the regulatory clarity and custody safety of an exchange-traded product to holding coins on a personal exchange account.

The fund is not for everyone. Cryptocurrency is speculative and unproven, subject to regulatory uncertainty. Altcoins — projects newer and smaller than Bitcoin — carry higher volatility and [concentration risk](/concentration-risk/). Many never survive. Investors should be comfortable with large short-term drawdowns.

## Real risks

The core risk is selectivity. By excluding Bitcoin and Ethereum, DIME bets that returns will be dispersed across smaller projects. But in many cycles the opposite is true: during downturns, [capital flows](/capital-flows/) toward the two safest names, leaving altcoins hit harder. In busts, concentration of losses in smaller projects has been severe.

Custody and [operational risk](/operational-risk/) is second. The cryptographic keys controlling the underlying assets are held by third-party [custodians](/custodian/). While these are reputable firms, the custody ecosystem is young and insurance coverage remains spotty. A custody failure could impair assets.

Liquidity of the underlying tokens is third. If many altcoins cannot be easily bought or sold on global exchanges, the fund's ability to rebalance or let shareholders redeem could be constrained. During market stress, liquidity vanishes.

Regulatory risk is final. Governments are still determining how to treat cryptocurrencies. A major jurisdiction could ban altcoin trading, restrict custody arrangements, or classify certain tokens as unregistered securities — any of which would impair the fund.

## How to research it

Start with the fund's prospectus and fact sheet, which spell out the exact index methodology and holdings. Check the most recent [net asset value](/net-asset-value/) and compare it to the spot prices of underlying tokens — a large premium or discount suggests structural issues. Look at the bid-ask spread during typical market hours to understand trading costs.

For ecosystem context, follow announcements from major platforms included in the fund and track regulatory news. Watch DIME's correlation with Bitcoin during market stress — that shows whether [diversification](/diversification/) is real or illusory. The fund publishes holdings and replication process; understanding how closely it tracks its target guides whether it works for your goals.
