Bitwise Avalanche ETF (BAVA) is an exchange-traded fund launched by Bitwise Asset Management that invests exclusively in Avalanche, the native token of the Avalanche blockchain. The fund trades on [NYSE Arca](/nyse-arca/) under the ticker BAVA and began trading in April 2026. Unlike traditional ETFs that track stocks or [bonds](/bond/), BAVA holds cryptocurrency directly and is not registered under the [Investment Company Act of 1940](/investment-company-act-of-1940/), meaning it operates outside the standard [mutual fund](/mutual-fund/) regulatory framework and does not receive the protections that registered investment companies provide.

The fund's principal innovation is its approach to staking. Bitwise has integrated staking—a mechanism by which cryptocurrency holders earn rewards by locking their tokens into a network—directly into the ETF structure. Rather than holding Avalanche tokens passively, BAVA stakes approximately 70 percent of its assets through Bitwise Onchain Solutions, the company's in-house staking division. This generates additional yield for ETF shareholders on top of any appreciation or [depreciation](/depreciation/) in the underlying token price. The fund maintains a 30 percent liquidity reserve to handle redemptions and provide operational flexibility without forcing the sale of staked tokens at inopportune times.

Avalanche is a blockchain platform designed to compete with [Ethereum](/ethereum/) and other smart-contract platforms by offering faster transaction finality, lower costs, and a different approach to network consensus. The Avalanche token (AVAX) serves multiple functions: it is used to pay transaction fees on the network, it is staked by validators who secure the network, and it is the asset in which the network stores value. As an investor in BAVA, you are indirectly investing in the success and adoption of the Avalanche blockchain ecosystem. Your returns depend on whether Avalanche grows in utility and popularity, which would increase demand for AVAX.

When BAVA launched, Bitwise waived the ETF's annual [management fee](/management-fee/) (ordinarily 0.34 percent) until either one month had elapsed or the fund reached $500 million in assets, whichever came first. At 0.34 percent, BAVA's fee is among the lowest available for single-token crypto ETFs, reflecting competition in the space and Bitwise's economies of scale. Once the promotional period ended, the standard fee began accruing, deducted daily from the fund's [net asset value](/net-asset-value/).

Investing in a single-token [cryptocurrency ETF](/cryptocurrency-etf/) concentrates risk substantially. Your entire return depends on the price movement of one token and the stability of one blockchain network. Unlike a diversified cryptocurrency fund or an equity [index fund](/index-fund/), you have no [diversification](/diversification/) across assets. If Avalanche faces technical problems, regulatory trouble, or competitive pressure from other blockchains, AVAX could decline sharply. Additionally, cryptocurrency markets are volatile and can move 20, 30, or 50 percent in days. The fact that BAVA stakes a portion of its holdings introduces additional complexity: if the staking contract encounters a bug or the network experiences a failure, staked tokens could be at risk, and liquidity constraints could delay redemptions if many shareholders want to exit simultaneously.

The regulatory landscape for crypto ETFs remains unsettled. BAVA is not a registered mutual fund and therefore does not receive certain investor protections, such as custody guarantees and segregation of assets. Bitwise, as the fund sponsor, is responsible for custody, but the level of legal recourse available to shareholders if Bitwise defaults or mismanages assets is less clear than with registered funds. The SEC has allowed spot [bitcoin](/bitcoin/) and ethereum ETFs to trade in recent years, but policy around more specialized crypto assets and staking mechanisms continues to evolve.

For investors considering BAVA, the core questions are whether you believe in Avalanche's technical advantages and long-term adoption, whether you accept the [concentration risk](/concentration-risk/) of a single-token fund, and whether you understand the additional complexity introduced by staking—not just the potential yield, but the liquidity and technical risks. BAVA is a vehicle for those who already have conviction around Avalanche and want direct, leveraged exposure via an ETF wrapper. It is not a vehicle for general cryptocurrency exposure or for investors who view crypto as a speculative bet.

To research BAVA and the Avalanche ecosystem, start by reviewing Bitwise's prospectus, which details the fund's holdings, staking mechanics, fees, and risk disclosures. Monitor on-chain metrics for Avalanche—validator count, transaction volume, application ecosystem size—to assess whether network growth is accelerating or stalling. Track the price of AVAX relative to other blockchain tokens and to Bitcoin and Ethereum. Finally, stay informed about regulatory developments: any SEC action against Bitwise, changes to the status of crypto securities, or shifts in staking regulation would directly impact the fund.
